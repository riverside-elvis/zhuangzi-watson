## 11 在宥 Let It Be, Leave It Alone

聞在宥天下
I have heard of letting the world be, of leaving it alone;
不聞治天下也
I have never heard of governing the world.

在之也者恐天下之淫其性也
You let it be for fear of corrupting the inborn nature of the world;
宥之也者恐天下之遷其德也
you leave it alone for fear of distracting the Virtue of the world.

天下不淫其性
不遷其德
有治天下者哉
If the nature of the world is not corrupted,
if the Virtue of the world is not distracted,
why should there be any governing of the world?

***

昔堯之治天下也使天下欣欣焉
Long ago, when the sage Yao governed the world, he made the world bright and gleeful;
人樂其性是不恬也
men delighted in their nature, and there was no calmness anywhere.

桀之治天下也使天下瘁瘁焉
When the tyrant Jie governed the world, he made the world weary and vexed;
人苦其性是不愉也
men found bitterness in their nature, and there was no contentment anywhere.

夫不恬不愉非德也
非德也而可長久者天下無之
To lack calmness, to lack contentment, is to go against Virtue,
and there has never been anyone in the world who could go against Virtue and survive for long.

***

人大喜邪
Are men exceedingly joyful?---
毗於陽
they will do damage to the yang element.

大怒邪
Are men exceedingly angry?---
毗於陰
they will do damage to the yin.

陰陽並毗四時不至
And when both yang and yin are damaged, the four seasons will not come as they should;
寒暑之和不成
heat and cold will fail to achieve their proper harmony;
其反傷人之形乎
and this in turn will do harm to the bodies of men.

使人喜怒失位
居處無常
思慮不自得
中道不成章
It will make men lose a proper sense of joy and anger,
to be constantly shifting from place to place,
to think up schemes that gain nothing,
to set out on roads that reach no glorious conclusion.

於是乎
天下始喬詰卓鷙
而後有盜跖曾史之行
Then for the first time,
the world will grow restless and aspiring,[^11-1]
and soon afterward will appear the ways of Robber Zhi, Zeng, and Shi.

[^11-1]: The words "restless and aspiring" represent four characters in the original whose meaning is very doubtful.

***

故舉天下以賞其善者不足
Then, although the whole world joins in rewarding good men, there will never be enough reward;
舉天下以罰其惡者不給
though the whole world joins in punishing evil men, there will never be enough punishment.

故天下之大
不足以賞罰
Huge as the world is,
it cannot supply sufficient reward or punishment.

自三代以下者
匈匈焉終以賞罰為事
From the Three Dynasties on down,
there has been nothing but bustle and fuss,
all over this matter of rewards and punishments.

彼何暇安其性命之情哉
How could people have any leisure to rest in the true form of their inborn nature and fate!

***

而且說明邪
Do men delight in what they see?---
是淫於色也
they are corrupted by colors.

說聰邪
Do they delight in what they hear?---
是淫於聲也
they are corrupted by sounds.

說仁邪
Do they delight in benevolence?---
是亂於德也
they bring confusion to Virtue.

說義邪
Do they delight in righteousness?---
是悖於理也
they turn their backs on reason.

說禮邪
Do they delight in rites?---
是相於技也
they are aiding artificiality.

說樂邪
Do they delight in music?---
是相於淫也
they are aiding dissolution.

Do they delight in sageness?---
說聖邪
they are assisting artifice.
是相於藝也

說知邪
Do they delight in knowledge?---
是相於疵也
they are assisting the fault finders.

天下將安其性命之情
之八者存可也亡可也
As long as the world rests in the true form of its in-born nature and fate,
it makes no difference whether or not these eight delights exist.

天下將不安其性命之情
之八者乃始臠卷獊囊
而亂天下也
But if the world does not rest in the true form of its nature and fate,
then these eight delights will begin to grow warped and crooked, jumbled and deranged,
and will bring confusion to the world.

而天下乃始尊之惜之
甚矣天下之惑也
And if on top of that, the world begins to honor them and cherish them,
then the delusion of the world will be great indeed!

豈直過也而去之邪
You say these are only a fancy that will pass in time?

乃齊戒以言之
跪坐以進之
鼓歌以儛之
Yet men prepare themselves with fasts and austerities when they come to describe them,
kneel solemnly on their mats when they recommend them,
beat drums and sing to set them forth in dance.

吾若是何哉
What's to be done about it, I'm sure I don't know!

***

故君子不得已而臨邪天下
莫若無為
If the gentleman finds he has no other choice than to direct and look after the world,
then the best course for him is inaction.

無為也
而後安其性命之情
As long as there is inaction,
he may rest in the true form of his nature and fate.

故貴以身於為天下
則可以託天下
If he values his own body more than the management of the world,
then he can be entrusted with the world.

愛以身於為天下
則可以寄天下
If he is more careful of his own body than of the management of the world,
then the world can be handed over to him.[^11-2]

[^11-2]: A similar saying is found in _Daodejing_ XIII, though the wording is somewhat different.

故君子苟能無解其五藏
無擢其聰明
尸居而龍見
淵默而雷聲
If the gentleman can in truth keep from rending apart his five vital organs,
from tearing out his eyesight and hearing,
then he will command corpse-like stillness and dragon vision,
the silence of deep pools, and the voice of thunder.

神動而天隨
從容無為
而萬物炊累焉
His spirit will move in the train of Heaven,
gentle and easy in inaction,
and the ten thousand things will be dust on the wind.

吾又何暇治天下哉
"What leisure have I now for governing the world?" he will say.

---

崔瞿問於老聃
Cui Zhu was questioning Lao Dan.

曰不治天下
安藏人心
"If you do not govern the world,
then how can you improve men's minds?"

***

老聃曰汝慎無攖人心
Lao Dan said, "Be careful---don't meddle with men's minds!

人心排下而進上
上下囚殺
Men's minds can be forced down or boosted up,
but this downing and upping imprisons and brings death to the mind.

淖約柔乎剛強
Gentle and shy, the mind can bend the hard and strong;
廉劌彫琢
it can chisel and cut away, carve and polish.

其熱焦火
其寒凝冰
其疾俛仰之間
而再撫四海之外
Its heat is that of burning fire,
its coldness that of solid ice,
its swiftness such that, in the time it takes to lift and lower the head,
it has twice swept over the four seas and beyond.

其居也淵而靜
At rest, it is deep fathomed and still;
其動也縣而天
僨驕而不可係者
in movement, it is far-flung as the heavens,
racing and galloping out of reach of all bonds.

其唯人心乎
This indeed is the mind of man!"

---

昔者黃帝始以仁義攖人之心
In ancient times the Yellow Emperor first used benevolence and righteousness to meddle with the minds of men.[^11-3]

[^11-3]: Daoist writers ordinarily have only praise for the Yellow Emperor,
and in Han times Daoism was known as *Huanglao*, the teaching of the Yellow Emperor and Laozi.
It is surprising, therefore, to find him cited here as the prime meddler,
though this is typical of the shifting roles assigned to the figures who appear in the _Zhuangzi_.
It is unclear whether the following section should be taken as a continuation
of Laozi's speech or as the words of the writer; I have taken it as the latter.

堯舜於是乎股無胈
脛無毛
以養天下之形
Yao and Shun followed him and worked till there was no more down on their thighs,
no more hair on their shins,
trying to nourish the bodies of the men of the world.

愁其五藏以為仁義
矜其血氣以規法度
They grieved their five vital organs in the practice of benevolence and righteousness,
taxed their blood and breath in the establishment of laws and standards.

然猶有不勝也
堯於是放讙兜於崇山
投三苗於三峗
流共工於幽都
But still some men would not submit to their rule,
and so they had to exile Huan Dou to Mount Chung,
drive away the Sanmiao tribes to the region of Sanwei,
and banish Gong to the Dark City.[^11-4]

[^11-4]: These banishments of evil and insubordinate men are mentioned in the _Book of Documents_,
"Canon of Shun," in which their presence has long raised the troubling question
of why there should have been any unsubmissive men during the rule of a sage.

此不勝天下也夫
This shows that they could not make the world submit.

***

施及三王
而天下大駭矣
By the time the kings of the Three Dynasties appeared,
the world was in great consternation indeed.

下有桀跖
上有曾史
而儒墨畢起
On the lowest level, there were men like the tyrant Jie and Robber Zhi,
on the highest, men like Zeng and Shi,
and the Confucianists and Mohists rose up all around.

於是乎喜怒相疑
Then joy and anger eyed each other with suspicion;
愚知相欺
stupidity and wisdom duped each other;
善否相非
good and bad called one another names;
誕信相譏
falsehood and truth slandered each other;
而天下衰矣
and the world sank into a decline.

大德不同
而性命爛漫矣
There was no more unity to the Great Virtue,
and the inborn nature and fate shattered and fell apart.

天下好知
而百姓求竭矣
The world coveted knowledge,
and the hundred clans were thrown into turmoil.[^11-5]

[^11-5]: Following Zhang Binglin's interpretation.

於是乎釿鋸制焉
Then there were axes and saws to shape things;
繩墨殺焉
ink and plumb lines to trim them;
椎鑿決焉
mallets and gouges to poke holes in them;
天下脊脊大亂
and the world, muddled and deranged, was in great confusion.

罪在攖人心
The crime lay in this meddling with men's minds.

故賢者伏處大山嵁巖之下
而萬乘之君憂慄乎廟堂之上
So it was that worthy men crouched in hiding below the great mountains and yawning cliffs,
and the lords of ten thousand chariots fretted and trembled above in their ancestral halls.

***

今世殊死者相枕也
In the world today, the victims of the death penalty lie heaped together;
桁楊者相推也
the bearers of cangues tread on one another's heels;
刑戮者相望也
the sufferers of punishment are never out of one another's sight.

而儒墨乃始離跂攘臂乎桎梏之間
And now come the Confucianists and Mohists,
waving their arms, striding into the very midst of the fettered and manacled men.

意甚矣哉
其無愧而不知恥也甚矣
Ah, that they should go this far,
that they should be so brazen, so lacking in any sense of shame!

吾未知聖知之不為桁楊椄槢也
仁義之不為桎梏鑿枘也
Who can convince me that sagely wisdom is not in fact the wedge that fastens the cangue,
that benevolence and righteousness are not in fact the loop and lock of these fetters and manacles?

焉知曾史之不為桀跖嚆矢也
How do I know that Zeng and Shi are not the whistling arrows that signal the approach of Jie and Zhi?

故曰絕聖棄知而天下大治
Therefore I say, cut off sageness, cast away wisdom, and the world will be in perfect order.

---

黃帝立為天子十九年
令行天下
聞廣成子在於空同之上
The Yellow Emperor had ruled as Son of Heaven for nineteen years,
and his commands were heeded throughout the world,
when he heard that Master Guang Cheng was living on top of the Mountain of Emptiness and Identity.

故往見之
He therefore went to visit him.

曰我聞吾子達於至道
"I have heard that you, sir, have mastered the Perfect Way.

敢問至道之精
May I venture to ask about the essence of the Perfect Way?" he said.

吾欲取天地之精以佐五穀
以養民人
"I would like to get hold of the essence of Heaven and earth and use it to aid the five grains
and to nourish the common people.

吾又欲官陰陽以遂群生
I would also like to control the yin and yang in order to ensure the growth of all living things.

為之奈何
How may this be done?"

***

廣成子曰而所欲問者物之質也
而所欲官者物之殘也
Master Guang Cheng said, "What you say you want to learn about pertains to the true substance of things,
but what you say you want to control pertains to things in their divided state.[^11-6]

[^11-6]: That is, the yin and yang, being two, already represent a departure from the primal unity of the Way.
What Master Guang Cheng is objecting to, of course, is the fact that the Yellow Emperor wishes to "control" them.

自而治天下
雲氣不待族而雨
Ever since you began to govern the world,
rain falls before the cloud vapors have even gathered;
草木不待黃而落
the plants and trees shed their leaves before they have even turned yellow;
日月之光益以荒矣
and the light of the sun and moon grows more and more sickly.

而佞人之心翦翦者
又奚足以語至道
Shallow and vapid, with the mind of a prattling knave---
what good would it do to tell *you* about the Perfect Way!"

***

黃帝退
捐天下
築特室
席白茅
閒居三月
The Yellow Emperor withdrew,
gave up his throne,
built a solitary hut,
spread a mat of white rushes,
and lived for three months in retirement.

復往邀之
Then he went once more to request an interview.

廣成子南首而臥
Master Guang Cheng was lying with his face to the south.[^11-7]

[^11-7]: The Chinese ruler, when acting as sovereign, faces south.
Master Guang Cheng, by assuming the same position, indicates his spiritual supremacy.

黃帝順下風膝行而進
再拜稽首而問曰聞吾子達於至道
The Yellow Emperor, approaching in a humble manner, crept forward on his knees,
bowed his head twice, and said, "I have heard that you, sir, have mastered the Perfect Way.

敢問治身奈
I venture to ask about the governing of the body.

何而可以長久
What should I do in order to live a long life?"

***

廣成子蹶然而起
Master Guang Cheng sat up with a start.

曰善哉問乎
"Excellent, this question of yours!

來吾語女至道
Come, I will tell you about the Perfect Way.

至道之精窈窈冥冥
The essence of the Perfect Way is deep and darkly shrouded;
至道之極昏昏默默
the extreme of the Perfect Way is mysterious and hushed in silence.

無視無聽
Let there be no seeing, no hearing;
抱神以靜
形將自正
enfold the spirit in quietude,
and the body will right itself.

必靜必清
無勞女形
無搖女精
乃可以長生
Be still, be pure,
do not labor your body,
do not churn up your essence,
and then you can live a long life.

目無所見
耳無所聞
心無所知
女神將守形
形乃長生
When the eye does not see,
the ear does not hear,
and the mind does not know,
then your spirit will protect the body,
and the body will enjoy long life.

慎女內
Be wary of what is within you;
閉女外
多知為敗
block off what is outside you,
for much knowledge will do you harm.

我為女遂於大明之上矣
至彼至陽之原也
Then I will lead you up above the Great Brilliance
to the source of the Perfect Yang;
為女入於窈冥之門矣
至彼至陰之原也
I will guide you through the Dark and Mysterious Gate
to the source of the Perfect Yin.

天地有官
陰陽有藏
Heaven and earth have their controllers,
the yin and yang their storehouses.

慎守女身
You have only to take care and guard your own body;
物將自壯
these other things will of themselves grow sturdy.

我守其一
以處其和
故我修身千二百歲矣
吾形未嘗衰
As for myself, I guard this unity,
abide in this harmony,
and therefore I have kept myself alive for twelve hundred years,
and never has my body suffered any decay."

***

黃帝再拜稽首曰廣成子之謂天矣
The Yellow Emperor bowed twice and said, "Master Guang Cheng, you have been as a Heaven to me!"

***

廣成子曰來吾語女
Master Guang Cheng said, "Come, I will explain to you.

彼其物無窮
而人皆以為有終
This Thing I have been talking about is inexhaustible,
and yet men all suppose that it has an end.

彼其物無測
而人皆以為有極
This Thing I have been talking about is unfathomable,
and yet men all suppose that it has a limit.

得吾道者上為皇
而下為王
He who attains my Way will be a Bright One on high,[^11-8]
and a king in the world below.

[^11-8]: The term "Bright One" (*huang*) was originally an epithet for Heaven
or a being commanding respect and awe, such as the sage rulers of antiquity.

失吾道者
上見光
而下為土
But he who fails to attain my Way,
though he may see the light above him,
will remain below as dust.

今夫百昌皆生於土而反於土
All the hundred creatures that flourish are born out of dust and return to dust.

故余將去女
入無窮之門以遊無極之野
吾與日月參光
吾與天地為常
So I will take leave of you,
to enter the gate of the inexhaustible and wander in the limitless fields,
to form a triad with the light of the sun and moon,
to partake in the constancy of Heaven and earth.

當我緡乎
What stands before me I mingle with,
遠我昏乎
what is far from me I leave in darkness.[^11-9]

[^11-9]: The meaning is doubtful.

人其盡死
All other men may die;
而我獨存乎
I alone will survive!"

---

雲將東遊
過扶搖之枝
而適遭鴻蒙
Cloud Chief was traveling east
and had passed the branches of the Fuyao
when he suddenly came upon Big Concealment.[^11-10]

[^11-10]: Cloud Chief and Big Concealment are inventions of the writer,
the latter apparently representing the Daoist sage. Fuyao appeared in sec. 1 as a name for the whirlwind;
here perhaps it is an error for Fusang, a huge mythical tree in the eastern sea from whose branches the sun rises.

鴻蒙方將拊髀雀躍而遊
Big Concealment at the moment was amusing himself by slapping his thighs and hopping around like a sparrow.

雲將見之
倘然止
贄然立曰叟何人邪
When Cloud Chief saw this,
he stopped in bewilderment,
stood dead still in his tracks, and said, "Old gentleman, who are you?

叟何為此
What is this you're doing?"

***

鴻蒙拊髀雀躍不輟
對雲將曰遊
Big Concealment, without interrupting his thigh slapping and sparrow hopping,
replied to Cloud Chief, "Amusing myself."

雲將曰朕願有問也
"I would like to ask a question," said Cloud Chief.

***

鴻蒙仰而視雲將曰吁
"Oh dear!" said Big Concealment, for the first time raising his head and looking at Cloud Chief.

***

雲將曰天氣不合
"The breath of heaven is out of harmony;
地氣鬱結
the breath of earth tangles and snarls," said Cloud Chief.

六氣不調
"The six breaths do not blend properly;[^11-11]
四時不節
the four seasons do not stay in order.

[^11-11]: Traditionally defined as the breaths of the yin, yang, wind, rain, darkness, and light.

今我願合六氣之精
以育群生
Now I would like to harmonize the essences of the six breaths
in order to bring nourishment to all living creatures.

為之奈何
How should I go about it?"

***

鴻蒙拊髀雀躍掉頭
Big Concealment, still thigh slapping and sparrow hopping, shook his head.

曰吾弗知
"I have no idea!

吾弗知
I have no idea!"

***

雲將不得問
So Cloud Chief got no answer.

又三年東遊
過有宋之野
而適遭鴻蒙
Three years later he was again traveling east and,
as he passed the fields of Song,
happened on Big Concealment once more.

雲將大喜
行趨而進曰天忘朕邪
Cloud Chief, overjoyed,
dashed forward and presented himself, saying, "Heavenly Master, have you forgotten me?

天忘朕邪
Have you forgotten me?"

再拜稽首
願聞於鴻蒙
Then he bowed his head twice
and begged for some instruction from Big Concealment.

***

鴻蒙曰浮游不知所求
Big Concealment said, "Aimless wandering does not know what it seeks;
猖狂不知所往
demented drifting does not know where it goes.

遊者鞅掌
以觀無妄
A wanderer, idle, unbound,
I view the sights of Undeception.

朕又何知
What more do I know?"

***

雲將曰朕也自以為猖狂
而百姓隨予所往
朕也不得已於民
Cloud Chief said, "I, too, consider myself a demented drifter,
but the people follow me wherever I go,
and I have no choice but to think of them.

今則民之放也願聞一言
It is for their sake now that I beg one word of instruction!"

***

鴻蒙曰亂天之經
逆物之情
玄天弗成
Big Concealment said, "If you confuse the constant strands of Heaven
and violate the true form of things,
then Dark Heaven will reach no fulfillment.

解獸之群
Instead, the beasts will scatter from their herds;
而鳥皆夜鳴
the birds will cry all night;
災及草木
disaster will come to the grass and trees;
禍及止蟲
misfortune will reach even to the insects.

意治人之過也
Ah, this is the fault of men who 'govern'!"

雲將曰然則吾奈何
"Then what should I do?" said Cloud Chief.

***

鴻蒙曰意毒哉
"Ah," said Big Concealment, "you are too far gone!

僊僊乎歸矣
Up, up, stir yourself and be off!"

***

雲將曰吾遇天難
Cloud Chief said, "Heavenly Master,
it has been hard indeed for me to meet with you---
願聞一言
I beg one word of in struction!"

***

鴻蒙曰意
"Well, then---
心養
mind-nourishment!" said Big Concealment.[^11-12]

[^11-12]: "Mind-nourishment" may seem an odd thing to recommend,
particularly as the whole anecdote is directed against purposeful "governing" or "nourishing."
But this is typical of Daoist paradox.
As we soon see, it does not in fact mean what it seems to mean.

汝徒處無為
而物自化
"You have only to rest in inaction,
and things will transform themselves.

墮爾形體
吐爾聰明
倫與物忘
大同乎涬溟
Smash your form and body,
spit out hearing and eyesight,
forget you are a thing among other things,
and you may join in great unity with the deep and boundless.

解心釋神
莫然無魂
萬物云云各復其根
Undo the mind, slough off spirit,
be blank and soulless,
and the ten thousand things one by one will return to the root---
各復其根而不知
return to the root and not know why.

渾渾沌沌
Dark and undifferentiated chaos---
終身不離
to the end of life, none will depart from it.

若彼知之
乃是離之
But if you try to know it,
you have already departed from it.

無問其名
Do not ask what its name is;
無闚其情
do not try to observe its form.

物故自生
Things will live naturally and of themselves."

***

雲將曰天降朕以德
示朕以默
Cloud Chief said, "The Heavenly Master has favored me with this Virtue,
instructed me in this Silence.

躬身求之
乃今也得
All my life I have been looking for it,
and now at last I have it!"

再拜稽首
起辭而行
He bowed his head twice, stood up,
took his leave, and went away.

---

世俗之人皆喜人之同乎己
而惡人之異於己也
The common run of men all welcome those who are like themselves
and scorn those who differ from themselves.

同於己而欲之
異於己而不欲者
以出乎眾為心也
The reason they favor those who are like themselves
and do not favor those who are different
is that their minds are set on distinguishing themselves from the crowd.

夫以出於眾為心者
曷嘗出乎眾哉
But if their minds are set on distinguishing themselves from the crowd,
how is this ever going to distinguish them from the crowd?

因眾以寧
所聞不如眾技眾矣
It is better to follow the crowd and be content,
for no matter how much you may know, it can never match the many talents of the crowd combined.

***

而欲為人之國者
Here is a man who wants to take over the management of another man's state.[^11-13]

[^11-13]: Probably a reference to the itinerant statesmen-advisers of late Zhou times
who wandered about offering their services to the various feudal lords.

此攬乎三王之利
而不見其患者也
He thinks thereby to seize all the profits enjoyed by the kings of the Three Dynasties
but fails to take note of their worries.

此以人之國僥倖也
幾何僥倖而不喪人之國乎
This is to gamble with another man's state,
and how long can you expect to gamble with his state and not lose it?

其存人之國也無萬分之一
Fewer than one man in ten thousand will succeed in holding on to the state;
而喪人之國也一不成而萬有餘喪矣
the odds in favor of losing it are more than ten thousand to one.

悲夫有土者之不知也
It is sad indeed that the possessors of states do not realize this!

***

夫有土者有大物也
Now the possessor of a state possesses a great thing.

有大物者
不可以物
Because he possesses a great thing,
he cannot be regarded as a mere thing himself.[^11-14]

[^11-14]: I follow Fukunaga in punctuating after the first *wu*.

物而不物
He is a thing, and yet he is not a mere thing;

故能物物
therefore he can treat other things as mere things.

明乎物物者之
非物也
He who clearly understands that in treating other things as mere things,
he himself is no longer a mere thing---
豈獨治天下百姓而已哉
how could he be content only to govern the hundred clans of the world and do nothing more?

出入六合
遊乎九州
獨往獨來
He will move in and out of the Six Realms,
wander over the Nine Continents,
going alone, coming alone.

是謂獨有
獨有之人是謂至貴
He may be called a Sole Possessor,
and a man who is a Sole Possessor may be said to have reached the peak of eminence.

***

大人之教若形之於影
聲之於響
The Great Man in his teaching is like the shadow that follows a form,
the echo that follows a sound.

有問而應之
盡其所懷
為天下配
Only when questioned does he answer,
and then he pours out all his thoughts,
making himself the companion of the world.

處乎無響
行乎無方
挈汝適復之撓撓
以遊無端
He dwells in the echoless,
moves in the directionless,
takes by the hand you who are rushing and bustling back and forth[^11-15]
and proceeds to wander in the beginningless.

[^11-15]: Following Yu Yue's interpretation.

出入無旁
與日無始
He passes in and out of the boundless
and is ageless as the sun.

頌論形軀
合乎大同
大同而無己
His face and form[^11-16]
blend with the Great Unity,
the Great Unity that is selfless.

[^11-16]: Following Zhang Binglin's interpretation.

無己
惡乎得有有
Being selfless,
how then can he look on possession as possession?

睹有者
He who fixed his eyes on possession---
昔之君子
he was the "gentleman" of ancient times.

睹無者
He who fixes his eyes on nothingness---
天地之友
he is the true friend of Heaven and earth.

---

賤而不可不任者
What is lowly and yet must be used---
物也
things.[^11-17]

[^11-17]: The remainder of the chapter, with its recognition of the necessity for
benevolence, righteousness, law, ritual, etc., seems to clash violently with what has gone before.
Some commentators interpret it as a description of the kind of compromise
that even the perfect Daoist ruler must make if he is to rule effectively.
Others regard it as an interpolation or a passage misplaced from some other section.
See the similar passage on p. 79.

卑而不可不因者
What is humble and yet must be relied on---
民也
the people.

匿而不可不為者
What is irksome[^11-18] and yet must be attended to---
事也
affairs.

[^11-18]: Following Ma Xulun's interpretation.

麤而不可不陳者
What is sketchy and yet must be proclaimed---
法也
laws.

遠而不可不居者
What seems to apply only to distant relationships and yet must be observed---
義也
righteousness.

親而不可不廣者
What seems to apply only to intimate relationships and yet must be broadened---
仁也
benevolence.

節而不可不積者
What is confining and yet must be repeatedly practiced---
禮也
ritual.

中而不可不高者
What is already apt and yet must be heightened---
德也
Virtue.

一而不可不易者
What is One and yet must be adapted---
道也
the Way.

神而不可不為者
What is spiritual and yet must be put into action---
天也
Heaven.

***

故聖人觀於天而不助
Therefore the sage contemplates Heaven but does not assist it.

成於德而不累
He finds completion in Virtue but piles on nothing more.

出於道而不謀
He goes forth in the Way but does not scheme.

會於仁而不恃
He accords with benevolence but does not set great store by it.

薄於義而不積
He draws close to righteousness but does not labor over it.

應於禮而不諱
He responds to the demands of ritual and does not shun them.

接於事而不辭
He disposes of affairs and makes no excuses.

齊於法而不亂
He brings all to order with laws and allows no confusion.

恃於民而不輕
He depends on the people and does not make light of them.

因於物而不去
He relies on things and does not throw them aside.

物者莫足為也
而不可不為
Among things, there are none that are worth using,
and yet they must be used.

***

不明於天者不純於德
He who does not clearly understand Heaven will not be pure in Virtue.

不通於道者無自而可
He who has not mastered the Way will find himself without any acceptable path of approach.

不明於道者悲夫
He who does not clearly understand the Way is pitiable indeed!

***

何謂道
What is this thing called the Way?

有天道
有人道
There is the Way of Heaven and the way of man.

無為而尊者
To rest in inaction, and command respect---
天道也
this is the Way of Heaven.

有為而累者
To engage in action and become entangled in it---
人道也
this is the way of man.

主者天道也
The ruler is the Way of Heaven;
臣者人道也
his subjects are the way of man.

天道之與人道也相去遠矣
The Way of Heaven and the way of man are far apart.

不可不察也
This is something to consider carefully!
