## 20 山木 The Mountain Tree

### Zhuangzi and the worthless tree and the goose that won't cackle

莊子行於山中見大木
Zhuangzi was walking in the mountains when he saw a huge tree,
枝葉盛茂
its branches and leaves thick and lush.

伐木者止其旁而不取也
A wood-cutter paused by its side but made no move to cut it down.

問其故曰
When Zhuangzi asked the reason, he replied,
無所可用
"There's nothing it could be used for!"

莊子曰
Zhuangzi said,
此木以不材
"Because of its worthlessness,
得終其天年
this tree is able to live out the years Heaven gave it."

***

夫子出於山
Down from the mountain,
舍於故人之家
the Master stopped for a night at the house of an old friend.

故人喜命豎子殺鴈而烹之
The friend, delighted, ordered his son to kill a goose and prepare it.

豎子請曰其一能鳴其一不能鳴
"One of the geese can cackle and the other can't," said the son.

請奚殺
"May I ask, please, which I should kill?"

主人曰殺不能鳴者
"Kill the one that can't cackle," said the host.

***

明日弟子問於莊子
The next day Zhuangzi's disciples questioned him.

曰昨日山中之木
"Yesterday there was a tree on the mountain
以不材得終其天年
that gets to live out the years Heaven gave it because of its worthlessness.

今主人之鴈以不材死
Now there's our host's goose that gets killed because of its worthlessness.

先生將何處
What position would you take in such a case, Master?"

***

莊子笑曰
Zhuangzi laughed and said,
周將處夫材與不材之間
"I'd probably take a position halfway between worth and worthlessness.

材與不材之間
But halfway between worth and worthlessness,
似之而非也
though it might seem to be a good place, really isn't---
故未免乎累
you'll never get away from trouble there.

若夫乘道德而浮游則不然
It would be very different, though, if you were to climb up on the Way and its Virtue and go drifting and wandering,
無譽無訾
neither praised nor damned,
一龍一蛇
now a dragon, now a snake,
與時俱化
shifting with the times,
而無肯專為
never willing to hold to one course only.

一上一下
Now up, now down,
以和為量
taking harmony for your measure,
浮游乎萬物之祖
drifting and wandering with the ancestor of the ten thousand things,
物物而不物於物
treating things as things but not letting them treat you as a thing---
則胡可得而累邪
then how could you get into any trouble?

此黃帝神農之法則也
This is the rule, the method of Shennong and the Yellow Emperor.

***

若夫萬物之情
"But now, what with the forms of the ten thousand things
人倫之傳
and the codes of ethics handed down from man to man,
則不然
matters don't proceed in this fashion.

合則離
Things join only to part,
成則毀
reach completion only to crumble.

廉則挫
If sharp edged, they are blunted;
尊則議
if high stationed, they are overthrown;[^20-1]
有為則虧
if ambitious, they are foiled.

[^20-1]: Following the emendation suggested by Yu Yue.
The word "things" in this passage includes mankind.

賢則謀
Wise, they are schemed against;
不肖則欺
stupid, they are swindled.

胡可得而必乎哉
What is there, then, that can be counted on?

悲夫
Only one thing, alas!---
弟子志之
remember this, my students---
其唯道德之鄉乎
only the realm of the Way and its Virtue!"

### Yiliao from south of the Market tells the marquis of Lu how to avoid disaster (the emtpy boat)


市南宜僚見魯侯
Yiliao from south of the Market called on the marquis of Lu.[^20-2]

[^20-2]: Xiong Yiliao, a man of Chu, is mentioned in _Zuozhuan_, Duke Ai, sixteenth year (479 BCE);
the "marquis of Lu" is presumably the Duke Ai of Lu.

魯侯有憂色
The marquis had a very worried look on his face.

市南子曰君有憂色何也
"Why such a worried look?" asked the Master from south of the Market.

***

魯侯曰
The marquis of Lu said,
吾學先王之道
"I study the way of the former kings;
修先君之業
I do my best to carry on the achievements of the former rulers;
吾敬鬼尊賢
I respect the spirits, honor worthy men,
親而行之
draw close to them, follow their advice,
無須臾離居
and never for an instant leave their side.

然不免於患
And yet I can't seem to avoid disaster.

吾是以憂
That's why I'm so worried."

***

市南子曰
The Master from south of the Market said,
君之除患之術淺矣
"Your technique for avoiding disaster is a very superficial one.

夫豐狐文豹
The sleek-furred fox and the elegantly spotted leopard
棲於山林
dwell in the mountain forest
伏於巖穴
and crouch in the cliffside caves---
靜也
such is their quietude.

夜行晝居
They go abroad by night but lurk at home by day---
戒也
such is their caution.

雖飢渴隱約
Though hunger, thirst, and hardship press them,
猶旦胥疏於江湖之上而求食焉
they steal forth only one by one to seek food by the rivers and lakes---
定也
such is their forethought.[^20-3]

[^20-3]: That is, they never venture forth in groups.
I follow texts that read *qie* in place of *dan* and adopt Ma Xulun's interpretation of *xusu*.

然且不免於罔羅機辟之患
And yet they can't seem to escape the disaster of nets and traps.

是何罪之有哉
Where is the blame?

其皮為之災也
Their fur is their undoing.

今魯國
And this state of Lu---
獨非君之皮邪
is it not your coat of fur?

吾願君刳形去皮
So I would ask you to strip away your form, rid yourself of this fur,
洒心去欲
wash clean your mind, be done with desire,
而遊於無人之野
and wander in the peopleless fields.

***

南越有邑焉
"In Nanyue there is a city,
名為建德之國
and its name is The Land of Virtue Established.

其民愚而朴
Its people are foolish and naive,
少私而寡欲
few in thoughts of self, scant in desires.

知作而不知藏
They know how to make but not how to lay away;
與而不求其報
they give but look for nothing in return.

不知義之所適
They do not know what accords with right;
不知禮之所將
they do not know what conforms to ritual.

猖狂妄行
Uncouth, uncaring, they move recklessly---
乃蹈乎大方
and in this way they tread the path of the Great Method.

其生可樂
Their birth brings rejoicing,
其死可葬
their death a fine funeral.

吾願君去國捐俗
So I would ask you to discard your state, break away from its customs,
與道相輔而行
and, with the Way as your helper, journey there."

***

君曰
The ruler of Lu said,
彼其道遠而險
"The road there is long and perilous.

又有江山
Moreover, there are rivers and mountains between,
我無舟車
and I have no boat or carriage.

奈何
What can I do?"

***

市南子曰
The Master from south of the Market said,
君無形倨
"Be without imperiousness,
無留居
be without conventionality---
以為舟車
let this be your carriage."[^20-4]

[^20-4]: Meaning very doubtful; Ma Xulun opines that something has dropped out of the text.

***

君曰
But the ruler of Lu said,
彼其道幽遠而無人
"The road is dark and long, and there are no people there.

吾誰與為鄰
Who will be my companion on the way?

吾無糧
When I have no rations,
我無食
when I have nothing to eat,
安得而至焉
how will I be able to reach my destination?"

***

市南子曰
The Master from south of the Market said,
少君之費
"Make few your needs,
寡君之欲
lessen your desires,
雖無糧而乃足
and then you may get along even without rations.

君其涉於江而浮於海
You will ford the rivers and drift out on the sea.

望之
Gaze all you may---
而不見其崖
you cannot see its farther shore;
愈往
journey on and on---
而不知其所窮
you will never find where it ends.

送君者皆自崖而反
Those who came to see you off will all turn back from the shore and go home
君自此遠矣
while you move ever farther into the distance.

***

故有人者累
"He who possesses men will know hardship;
見有於人者憂
he who is possessed by men will know care.

故堯非有人
Therefore Yao neither possessed men
非見有於人也
nor allowed himself to be possessed by them.

吾願去君之累
So I ask you to rid yourself of hardship,
除君之憂
to cast off your cares,
而獨與道遊於大莫之國
and to wander alone with the Way to the Land of Great Silence.

***

方舟而濟於河
"If a man, having lashed two hulls together, is crossing a river,
有虛船來觸舟
and an empty boat happens along and bumps into him,
雖有惼心之人不怒
no matter how hot tempered the man may be, he will not get angry.

有一人在其上
But if there should be someone in the other boat,
則呼張歙之
then he will shout out to haul this way or veer that.

一呼而不聞
If his first shout is not heeded,
再呼而不聞
he will shout again, and if that is not heard,
於是三呼邪
he will shout a third time,
則必以惡聲隨之
this time with a torrent of curses following.

向也不怒
In the first instance, he wasn't angry;
而今也怒
now in the second, he is.

向也虛而今也實
Earlier he faced emptiness, now he faces occupancy.

人能虛己以遊世
If a man could succeed in making himself empty and, in that way, wander through the world,
其孰能害之
then who could do him harm?"

### Beigong She raises taxes for bells

北宮奢為衛靈公
Beigong She was collecting taxes for Duke Ling of Wei
賦斂以為鐘
in order to make a set of bells.

為壇乎國門之外
He built a platform outside the gate of the outer wall,
三月而成
and in the space of three months the bells were completed,
上下之縣
both the upper and lower tiers.[^20-5]

[^20-5]: There were sixteen bells in a set, arranged in two tiers.
Most commentators take the "platform" to be an altar on which a sacrifice was made
in preparation for the casting of the bells,
though Ma Xulun believes it was connected with the actual casting process.

王子慶忌見而問焉曰
Prince Qingji, observing this, asked,
子何術之設
"What art is it you wield?"[^20-6]

[^20-6]: Prince Qingji, son of King Liao of Wu,
had fled to the state of Wei to escape from his father's assassin and successor,
King Helü, who took the throne of Wu in 514 BCE.

***

奢曰
Beigong She replied,
一之間無敢設也
"In the midst of Unity, how should I venture to 'wield' anything?

奢聞之
I have heard it said,
既彫既琢
When carving and polishing are done,
復歸於朴
then return to plainness.

侗乎其無識
Dull, I am without understanding;
儻乎其怠疑
placid, I dawdle and drift.

萃乎芒乎
Mysteriously, wonderfully
其送往而迎來
I bid farewell to what goes, I greet what comes;
來者勿禁
for what comes cannot be denied,
往者勿止
and what goes cannot be detained.

從其彊梁
I follow the rude and violent,
隨其曲傅
trail after the meek and bending,
因其自窮
letting each come to its own end.

故朝夕賦斂
So I can collect taxes from morning to night
而毫毛不挫
and meet not the slightest rebuff.

而況有大塗者乎
How much more would this be true, then, of a man who had hold of the Great Road?"

### Taigong Ren gets Confucius to retire to the great swamp

孔子圍於陳蔡之間
Confucius was besieged between Chen and Cai,
七日不火食
and for seven days he ate no cooked food.

大公任往弔之
Taigong Ren went to offer his sympathy.

曰子幾死乎
"It looks as if you're going to die," he said.

曰然
"It does indeed."

子惡死乎
"Do you hate the thought of dying?"

曰然
"I certainly do!"

***

任曰
Ren said,
予嘗言不死之道
"Then let me try telling you about a way to keep from dying.

東海有鳥焉
In the eastern sea,
其名曰意怠
there is a bird and its name is Listless.

其為鳥也翂翂翐翐
It flutters and flounces
而似無能
but seems to be quite helpless.

引援而飛
It must be boosted and pulled before it can get into the air,
迫脅而棲
pushed and shoved before it can get back to its nest.

進不敢為前
It never dares to be the first to advance,
退不敢為後
never dares to be the last to retreat.

食不敢先嘗
At feeding time, it never ventures to take the first bite
必取其緒
but picks only at the leftovers.

是故其行列不斥
So when it flies in file, it never gets pushed aside,
而外人卒不得害
nor do other creatures such as men ever do it any harm.

是以免於患
In this way, it escapes disaster.

***

直木先伐
"The straight-trunked tree is the first to be felled;
甘井先竭
the well of sweet water is the first to run dry.

子其意者
And you, now---
飾知以驚愚
you show off your wisdom in order to astound the ignorant,
修身以明汙
work at your good conduct in order to distinguish yourself from the disreputable,
昭昭乎若揭日月而行
going around bright and shining as though you were carrying the sun and moon in your hand!

故不免也
That's why you can't escape!

***

昔吾聞之大成之人曰
"I have heard the Man of Great Completion say:
自伐者無功
'Boasts are a sign of no success;
功成者墮
success once won faces overthrow;
名成者虧
fame once won faces ruin.'

孰能去功與名
Who can rid himself of success and fame,
而還與眾人
return and join the common run of men?

道流而不明居
His Way flows abroad, but he does not rest in brightness;
得行而不名處
his Virtue[^20-7] moves, but he does not dwell in fame.

[^20-7]: The text has the *de*, which means "gain,"
which may be either an error for the *de* meaning "virtue" or a deliberate play on the two words.
See p. 129, n. 8.

純純常常
Vacant, addled,
乃比於狂
he seems close to madness.

削跡捐勢
Wiping out his footprints, sloughing off his power,
不為功名
he does not work for success or fame.

是故無責於人
So he has no cause to blame other men,
人亦無責焉
nor other men to blame him.

至人不聞
The Perfect Man wants no repute.

子何喜哉
Why then do you delight in it so?"

***

孔子曰善哉
"Excellent!" exclaimed Confucius.

辭其交遊
Then he said goodbye to his friends and associates,
去其弟子
dismissed his disciples,
逃於大澤
and retired to the great swamp,
衣裘褐
wearing furs and coarse cloth
食杼栗
and living on acorns and chestnuts.

入獸不亂群
He could walk among the animals without alarming their herds,
入鳥不亂行
walk among the birds without alarming their flocks.

鳥獸不惡
If even the birds and beasts did not resent him,
而況人乎
how much less would men!

### Master Snaghu gets Confucius to abandon his studies and gives away his books

孔子問子桑雽曰
Confucius said to Master Sanghu,
吾再逐於魯
"Twice I have been driven out of Lu.

伐樹於宋
The people chopped down a tree on me in Song,
削跡於衛
wiped away my footprints in Wei,
窮於商周
made trouble for me in Shang and Zhou,
圍於陳蔡之間
and besieged me between Chen and Cai---
吾犯此數患
so many calamities have I encountered.

親交益疏
My kinfolk and associates drift further and further away;
徒友益散
my friends and followers one after the other take leave.

何與
Why is this?"

***

子桑雽曰
Master Sanghu said,
子獨不聞假
"Have you never heard about Lin Hui,
人之亡與
the man who fled from Jia?

林回棄千金之璧
He threw away his jade disk worth a thousand measures of gold,
負赤子而趨
strapped his little baby on his back, and hurried off.

或曰
Someone said to him,
為其布與
'Did you think of it in terms of money?

赤子之布寡矣
Surely a little baby isn't worth much money!

為其累與
Or were you thinking of the bother?

赤子之累多矣
But a little baby is a great deal of bother!

棄千金之璧
Why, then, throw away a jade disk worth a thousand measures of gold
負赤子而趨何也
and hurry off with a little baby on your back?'

***

林回曰
"Lin Hui replied,
彼以利合
'The jade disk and I were joined by profit,
此以天屬也
but the child and I were brought together by Heaven.

夫以利合者迫窮禍患害相棄也
When pressed by misfortune and danger, things joined by profit will cast one another aside;
以天屬者迫窮禍患害相收也
but when pressed by misfortune and danger, things brought together by Heaven will cling to one another.

夫相收之與相棄亦遠矣
To cling to one another and to cast one another aside are far apart indeed!'

***

且君子之交淡若水
"The friendship of a gentleman, they say, is insipid as water;
小人之交甘若醴
that of a petty man, sweet as rich wine.

君子淡以親
But the insipidity of the gentleman leads to affection,
小人甘以絕
while the sweetness of the petty man leads to revulsion.

彼無故以合者
Those with no particular reason for joining together
則無故以離
will, for no particular reason, part."

***

孔子曰
Confucius said,
敬聞命矣
"I will do my best to honor your instructions!"

徐行翔佯而歸
Then with leisurely steps and a free and easy manner, he returned home.

絕學捐書
He abandoned his studies, gave away his books,
弟子無挹於前
and his disciples no longer came to bow in obeisance before him,
其愛益加進
but their affection for him was greater than it had ever been before.

***

異日桑雽又曰
Another day Master Sanghu likewise said,
舜之將死
"When Shun was about to die,
真泠禹曰
he carefully[^20-8] instructed Yu in these words:
汝戒之哉
'Mark what I say!

[^20-8]: Reading *shen* (*zhen* with the heart radical).

形莫若緣
In the case of the body, it is best to let it go along with things.

情莫若率
In the case of the emotions, it is best to let them follow where they will.

緣則不離
By going along with things, you avoid becoming separated from them.

率則不勞
By letting the emotions follow as they will, you avoid fatigue.

不離不勞
And when there is no separation or fatigue,
則不求文以待形
then you need not seek any outward adornment or depend on the body.

不求文以待形
And when you no longer seek outward adornment or depend on the body,
固不待物
you have in fact ceased to depend on any material thing.'"

### Zhuangzi tells the king of Wei the parable of the distressed monkeys

莊子衣大布而補之
Zhuangzi put on his robe of coarse cloth with the patches on it,
正緳係履而過魏王
tied his shoes with hemp to keep them from falling apart, and went to call on the king of Wei.

魏王曰何先生之憊邪
"My goodness, sir, you certainly are in distress!" said the king of Wei.

***

莊子曰
Zhuangzi said,
貧也非憊也
"I am poor, but I am not in distress!

士有道德不能行憊也
When a man possesses the Way and its Virtue but cannot put them into practice, then he is in distress.

衣弊履穿貧也
When his clothes are shabby and his shoes worn through, then he is poor,
非憊也
but he is not in distress.

此所謂非遭時也
This is what they call being born at the wrong time.

王獨不見夫騰猿乎
Has Your Majesty never observed the bounding monkeys?

其得柟梓豫章也
If they can reach the tall cedars, the catalpas, or the camphor trees,
攬蔓其枝
they will swing and sway from their limbs,
而王長其間
frolic and lord it in their midst,
雖羿蓬蒙不能眄睨也
and even the famous archers Yi or Peng Meng could not take accurate aim at them.

及其得柘棘枳枸之閒也
But when they find themselves among prickly mulberries, brambles, hawthorns, or spiny citrons,
危行側視
they must move with caution,
振動悼慄
glancing from side to side, quivering and quaking with fear.

此筋骨非有加急而不柔也
It is not that their bones and sinews have suddenly become stiff and lost their suppleness.

處勢不便未足以逞其能也
It is simply that the monkeys find themselves in a difficult and disadvantageous position in which they cannot exercise their abilities to the full.

今處昏上亂相之間而欲無憊
And now if I should live under a benighted ruler and among traitorous ministers and still hope to escape distress,
奚可得邪
what hope would there be of doing so?

此比干之見剖心
Bi Gan had his heart cut out---
徵也夫
there is the proof of the matter!"[^20-9]

[^20-9]: On Prince Bi Gan, who was put to death by the tyrant Zhou, see p. 23.
Zhuangzi is presumably explaining why he does not take public office in the troubled times in which he lived.

### Confucius and Yan Hui wait for the end between Chen and Cai

孔子窮於陳蔡之間
Confucius was in trouble between Chen and Cai,
七日不火食
and for seven days he ate no cooked food.

左據槁木
His left hand propped against a withered tree,
右擊槁枝
his right beating time on a withered limb,
而歌猋氏之風
he sang the air of the lord of Yan.[^20-10]

[^20-10]: The lord of Yan appeared in sec. 14, p. 111;
he was presumably a sage ruler of antiquity, identified by some commentators with Shennong.
Some texts give his name as "the lord of Piao."

有其具而無其數
The rapping of the limb provided an accompaniment, but it was without any fixed rhythm;
有其聲而無宮角
there was melody, but none that fitted the usual tonal categories of *gong* or *jue*.

木聲與人聲犁然有當於人心
The drumming on the tree and the voice of the singer had a pathos to them that would strike a man's heart.

***

顏回端拱
Yan Hui, standing with hands folded respectfully across his chest,
還目而窺之
turned his eyes and looked inquiringly at Confucius.

仲尼恐其廣己而造大也
Confucius, fearful that Yan Hui's respect for him was too great,
愛己而造哀也曰
that his love for him was too tender, said to him,
回
"Hui!
無受天損易
It is easy to be indifferent to the afflictions of Heaven
無受人益難
but hard to be indifferent to the benefits of man.

無始而非卒也
No beginning but has its end,
人與天一也
and man and Heaven are one.

夫今之歌者其誰乎
Who is it, then, who sings this song now?"

***

回曰
Hui said,
敢問無受天損易
"May I venture to ask what you mean when you say it is easy to be indifferent to the afflictions of Heaven?"

***

仲尼曰
Confucius said,
飢溺寒暑
"Hunger, thirst, cold, heat,
窮桎不行
barriers and blind alleys that will not let you pass---
天地之行也
these are the workings of Heaven and earth,
運物之泄也
the shifts of ever turning things.

言與之偕逝之謂也
This is what is called traveling side by side with the others.

為人臣者不敢去之
He who serves as a minister does not dare to abandon his lord.

執臣之道猶若是
And if he is thus faithful to the way of a true minister,
而況乎所以待天乎
how much more would he be if he were to attend on Heaven!"

何謂無受人益難
"And what do you mean when you say that it is hard to be indifferent to the benefits of man?"

***

仲尼曰
Confucius replied,
始用四達
"A man sets out on a career, and soon he is advancing in all four directions at once.

爵祿並至而不窮
Titles and stipends come raining down on him without end,
物之所利乃非己也
but these are merely material profits and have nothing to do with the man himself.

吾命有在外者也
As for me, my fate lies elsewhere.

君子不為盜
A gentleman will not pilfer,
賢人不為竊
a worthy man will not steal.

吾若取之何哉
What business would I have, then, trying to acquire such things?

故曰
So it is said,
鳥莫知於鷾鴯
There is no bird wiser than the swallow.

目之所不宜處
If its eyes do not light on a suitable spot,
不給視
it will not give a second look.

雖落其實
If it happens to drop the food it had in its beak,
棄之而走
it will let it go and fly on its way.

其畏人也
It is wary of men,
而襲諸人間
and yet it lives among them,
社稷存焉爾
finding its protection along with men in the village altars of the soil and grain."

***

何謂
"And what do you mean by saying,
無始而非卒
'No beginning but has its end'?"

***

仲尼曰
Confucius said,
化其萬物
"There is a being who transforms the ten thousand things,
而不知其禪之者
yet we do not know how he works these changes.

焉知其所終
How do we know what is an end?

焉知其所始
How do we know what is a beginning?

正而待之而已耳
The only thing for us to do is just to wait!"

***

何謂
"And what do you mean by saying,
天與人一邪
'man and Heaven are one'?"

***

仲尼曰
Confucius said,
有人天也
"Man exists because of Heaven,
有天亦天也
and Heaven, too, exists because of Heaven.

人之不能有天
But man cannot cause Heaven to exist;
性也
this is because of [the limitations of] his inborn nature.

聖人晏然體逝而終矣
The sage, calm and placid, embodies change and so comes to his end."

### Zhuang Zhou and the magpie in the park at Diaoling

莊周遊乎雕陵之樊
Zhuang Zhou was wandering in the park at Diaoling
睹一異鵲自南方來者
when he saw a peculiar kind of magpie that came flying along from the south.

翼廣七尺
It had a wingspread of seven feet,
目大運寸
and its eyes were a good inch in diameter.

感周之顙而集於栗林
It brushed against Zhuang Zhou's forehead and then settled down in a grove of chestnut trees.

莊周曰此何鳥哉
"What kind of bird is that!" exclaimed Zhuang Zhou.

翼殷不逝
"Its wings are enormous, but they get it nowhere;
目大不覩
its eyes are huge, but it can't even see where it's going!"

蹇裳躩步
Then he hitched up his robe, strode forward,
執彈而留之
cocked his crossbow, and prepared to take aim.

睹一蟬方得美蔭
As he did so, he spied a cicada that had found a lovely spot of shade
而忘其身
and had forgotten all about [the possibility of danger to] its body.

螳蜋執翳而搏之
Behind it, a praying mantis, stretching forth its claws, prepared to snatch the cicada,
見得而忘其形
and it, too, had forgotten about its own form as it eyed its prize.

異鵲從而利之
The peculiar magpie was close behind, ready to make off with the praying mantis,
見利而忘其真
forgetting its own true self as it fixed its eyes on the prospect of gain.

莊周怵然曰
Zhuang Zhou, shuddering at the sight, said,
噫物固相累
"Ah!---things do nothing but make trouble for one another---
二類相召也
one creature calling down disaster on another!"

捐彈而反走
He threw down his crossbow, turned about, and hurried from the park,
虞人逐而誶之
but the park keeper [taking him for a poacher] raced after him with shouts of accusation.

***

莊周反入
Zhuang Zhou returned home and,
三月不庭
for three months, looked unhappy.[^20-11]

[^20-11]: Following Wang Niansun's emendation.

藺且從而問之
Lin Ju in the course of tending to his master's needs, questioned him, saying,
夫子何為頃間甚不庭乎
"Master, why is it that you are so unhappy these days?"

***

莊周曰
Zhuang Zhou said,
吾守形而忘身
"In clinging to outward form, I have forgotten my own body.

觀於濁水
Staring at muddy water,
而迷於清淵
I have been misled into taking it for a clear pool.

且吾聞諸夫子曰
Moreover, I have heard my Master say,
入其俗
'When you go among the vulgar,
從其俗
follow their rules!'

今吾遊於雕陵而忘吾身
I went wandering at Diaoling and forgot my body.

異鵲感吾顙
A peculiar magpie brushed against my forehead,
遊於栗林而忘真
wandered off to the chestnut grove, and there forgot its true self.

栗林虞人以吾為戮
And the keeper of the chestnut grove, to my great shame, took me for a trespasser!

吾所以不庭也
That is why I am unhappy."

### Yangzi and the innkeeper's concubines

陽子之宋
Yangzi, on his way to Song,
宿於逆旅
stopped for the night at an inn.

逆旅有妾二人
The innkeeper had two concubines,
其一人美
one beautiful,
其一人惡
the other ugly.

惡者貴
But the ugly one was treated as a lady of rank,
而美者賤
while the beautiful one was treated as a menial.

陽子問其故
When Yangzi asked the reason,
逆旅小子對曰
a young boy of the inn replied,
其美者自美
"The beautiful one is only too aware of her beauty,
吾不知其美也
so we don't think of her as beautiful.

其惡者自惡
The ugly one is only too aware of her ugliness,
吾不知其惡也
so we don't think of her as ugly."

***

陽子曰
Yangzi said,
弟子記之
"Remember that, my students!

行賢而去自賢之行
If you act worthily but rid yourself of the awareness that you are acting worthily,
安往而不愛哉
then where can you go that you will not be loved?"
