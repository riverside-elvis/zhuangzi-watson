## 18 至樂 Supreme Happiness

### Being happy and staying alive

天下有至樂無有哉
Is there such a thing as supreme happiness in the world, or isn't there?

有可以活身者無有哉
Is there some way to keep yourself alive, or isn't there?

今奚為奚據
What to do, what to rely on,
奚避奚處
what to avoid, what to stick by,
奚就奚去
what to follow, what to leave alone,
奚樂奚惡
what to find happiness in, what to hate?

***

夫天下之所尊者
This is what the world honors:
富
wealth,
貴
eminence,
壽
long life,
善也
a good name.

所樂者
This is what the world finds happiness in:
身安
a life of ease,
厚味
rich food,
美服
fine clothes,
好色
beautiful sights,
音聲也
sweet sounds.

所下者
This is what it looks down on:
貧
poverty,
賤
meanness,
夭
an early death,
惡也
a bad name.

所苦者
This is what it finds bitter:
身不得安逸
a life that knows no rest,
口不得厚味
a mouth that gets no rich food,
形不得美服
no fine clothes for the body,
目不得好色
no beautiful sights for the eye,
耳不得音聲
no sweet sounds for the ear.

***

若不得者
People who can't get these things
則大憂以懼
fret a great deal and are afraid---
其為形也亦愚哉
this is a stupid way to treat the body.

夫富者苦身疾作
People who are rich wear themselves out rushing around on business,
多積財而不得盡用
piling up more wealth than they could ever use---
其為形也亦外矣
this is a superficial way to treat the body.

夫貴者夜以繼日
People who are eminent spend night and day
思慮善否
scheming and wondering whether they are doing right---
其為形也亦疏矣
this is a shoddy way to treat the body.

人之生也與憂俱生
Man lives his life in company with worry,
壽者惛惛
and if he lives a long while till he's dull and doddering,
久憂不死
then he has spent that much time worrying instead of dying,
何苦也
a bitter lot indeed!

其為形也亦遠矣
This is a callous way to treat the body.

***

烈士為天下見善矣
Men of ardor[^18-1] are regarded by the world as good,
未足以活身
but their goodness doesn't succeed in keeping them alive.

[^18-1]: See p. 134, n. 15.

吾未知善之誠善邪
誠不善邪
So I don't know whether or not their goodness is really good.

若以為善矣
Perhaps I think it's good---
不足活身
but not good enough to save their lives.

以為不善矣
Perhaps I think it's no good---
足以活人
but still good enough to save the lives of others.

故曰
So I say,
忠諫不聽
if your loyal advice isn't heeded,
蹲循勿爭
give way and do not wrangle.

故夫子胥爭之以殘其形
Zixu wrangled and lost his body.[^18-2]

[^18-2]: Wu Zixu, minister to the king of Wu, repeatedly warned the king of the danger
of attack from the state of Yue. He finally aroused the king's ire and suspicion
and was forced to commit suicide in 484 BCE.

不爭
But if he hadn't wrangled,
名亦不成
he wouldn't have made a name.

誠有善無有哉
Is there really such a thing as goodness, or isn't there?

***

今俗之所為與其所樂
What ordinary people do and what they find happiness in---
吾又未知樂之果樂邪果不樂邪
I don't know whether or not such happiness is, in the end, really happiness.

吾觀夫俗之所樂
I look at what ordinary people find happiness in,
舉群趣者
what they all make a mad dash for,
誙誙然如將不得已
racing around as though they couldn't stop---
而皆曰樂者
they all say they're happy with it.

吾未之樂也
I'm not happy with it,
亦未之不樂也
and I'm not unhappy with it.

果有樂無有哉
In the end, is there really happiness, or isn't there?

***

吾以無為誠樂矣
I take inaction to be true happiness,
又俗之所大苦也
but ordinary people think it is a bitter thing.

故曰
I say:
至樂無樂
the highest happiness has no happiness,
至譽無譽
the highest praise has no praise.

天下是非果未可定也
The world can't decide what is right and what is wrong.

雖然無為可以定是非
And yet inaction can decide this.

至樂活身
The highest happiness, keeping alive---
唯無為幾存
only inaction gets you close to this!

***

請嘗試言之
Let me try putting it this way.

天無為以之清
The inaction of Heaven is its purity,
地無為以之寧
the inaction of earth is its peace.

故兩無為相合
So the two inactions combine,
萬物皆化
and all things are transformed and brought to birth.

芒乎芴乎
Wonderfully, mysteriously,
而無從出乎
there is no place they come out of.

芴乎芒乎
Mysteriously, wonderfully,
而無有象乎
they have no sign.

萬物職職
Each thing minds its business,
皆從無為殖
and all grow up out of inaction.

故曰
So I say,
天地無為也
Heaven and earth do nothing,
而無不為也
and there is nothing that is not done.

人也孰能得無為哉
Among men, who can get hold of this inaction?

---

### Zhuaungzi's wife dies

莊子妻死
Zhuangzi's wife died.

惠子弔之
When Huizi went to convey his condolences,
莊子則方箕
he found Zhuangzi sitting with his legs sprawled out,
踞鼓盆而歌
pounding on a tub and singing.

惠子曰與人居長子老身死
"You lived with her, she brought up your children and grew old," said Huizi.

不哭亦足矣
"It should be enough simply not to weep at her death.

又鼓盆而歌
But pounding on a tub and singing---
不亦甚乎
this is going too far, isn't it?"

***

莊子曰
Zhuangzi said,
不然
"You're wrong.

是其始死也
When she first died,
我獨何能無概然
do you think I didn't grieve like anyone else?

察其始而本無生
But I looked back to her beginning and the time before she was born.

非徒無生也
Not only the time before she was born,
而本無形
but the time before she had a body.

非徒無形也
Not only the time before she had a body,
而本無氣
but the time before she had a spirit.

雜乎芒芴之間
In the midst of the jumble of wonder and mystery,
變而有氣
a change took place and she had a spirit.

氣變而有形
Another change and she had a body.

形變而有生
Another change and she was born.

今又變而之死
Now there's been another change and she's dead.

是相與為春秋冬夏四時行也
It's just like the progression of the four seasons:
spring, summer, fall, winter.

***

人且偃然寢於巨室
"Now she's going to lie down peacefully in a vast room.

而我噭噭然隨而哭之
If I were to follow after her bawling and sobbing,
自以為不通乎命
it would show that I don't understand anything about fate.

故止也
So I stopped."

### Uncle Lame-Gait sprouts a willow

支離叔與滑介叔觀於冥伯之丘
崑崙之虛
黃帝之所休
Uncle Lack-Limb and Uncle Lame-Gait were seeing the sights at Dark Lord Hill
and the wastes of Kunlun,
the place where the Yellow Emperor rested.[^18-3]

[^18-3]: These all are places or persons associated in Chinese legend with immortality.
The Yellow Emperor, as we have seen on pp. 45–46, did not die but ascended to Heaven.

俄而柳生其左肘
Suddenly a willow sprouted out of Uncle Lame-Gait's left elbow.[^18-4]

[^18-4]: According to the more prosaic interpretation of Li Ciming,
the character for "willow" is a loan for the word "tumor."

其意蹶蹶然惡之
He looked very startled and seemed to be annoyed.

支離叔曰
"Do you resent it?"
子惡之乎
said Uncle Lack-Limb.

***

滑介叔曰亡予何惡
"No---what is there to resent?" said Uncle Lame-Gait.

生者假借也
"To live is to borrow.

假之而生
生者塵垢也
And if we borrow to live,
then life must be a pile of trash.

死生為晝夜
Life and death are day and night.

且吾與子觀化
而化及我
You and I came to watch the process of change,
and now change has caught up with me.

我又何惡焉
Why would I have anything to resent?"

### Zhuangzi questions a skull in Chu

莊子之楚
When Zhuangzi went to Chu,
見空髑髏
he saw an old skull,
髐然有形
all dry and parched.

撽以馬捶因而問之曰
He poked it with his carriage whip and then asked,
夫子貪生失理而為此乎
"Sir, were you greedy for life and forgetful of reason and so came to this?

將子有亡國之事
Was your state overthrown,
斧鉞之誅而為此乎
and did you bow beneath the ax and so came to this?

將子有不善之行
Did you do some evil deed,
愧遺父母妻子之醜而為此乎
and were you ashamed to bring disgrace on your parents and family and so came to this?

將子有凍餒之患而為此乎
Was it through the pangs of cold and hunger that you came to this?

將子之春秋故及此乎
Or did your springs and autumns pile up until they brought you to this?"

***

於是語卒
When he had finished speaking,
援髑髏枕而臥
he dragged the skull over and, using it for a pillow, lay down to sleep.

***

夜半
In the middle of the night,
髑髏見夢曰
the skull came to him in a dream and said,
子之談者似辯士
"You chatter like a rhetorician,
視子所言皆生人之累也
and all your words betray the entanglements of a living man.

死則無此矣
The dead know nothing of these!

子欲聞死之說乎
Would you like to hear a lecture on the dead?"

莊子曰然
"Indeed," said Zhuangzi.

***

髑髏曰
The skull said,
死
"Among the dead,
無君於上
there are no rulers above,
無臣於下
no subjects below,
亦無四時之事
and no chores of the four seasons.

從然以天地為春秋
With nothing to do, our springs and autumns are as endless as heaven and earth.

雖南面王樂不能過也
A king facing south on his throne could have no more happiness than this!"

***

莊子不信曰
Zhuangzi couldn't believe this and said,
吾使司命復生子形
"If I got the Arbiter of Fate to give you a body again,
為子骨肉肌膚
make you some bones and flesh,
反子父母妻子閭里知識
return you to your parents and family and your old home and friends,
子欲之乎
you would want that, wouldn't you?"

***

髑髏深矉
The skull frowned severely,
蹙頞
wrinkling up its brow.

曰吾安能棄南面王樂
"Why would I throw away more happiness than that of a king on a throne
而復為人間之勞乎
and take on the troubles of a human being again?" it said.

### Zigong asks Confucius why he is worried about Yan Yuan in Qi (the bird of Lu)

顏淵東之齊
When Yan Yuan went east to Qi,
孔子有憂色
Confucius had a very worried look on his face.[^18-5]

[^18-5]: Yan Yuan or Yan Hui, who appeared earlier, was Confucius's favorite disciple.

子貢下席而問曰
Zigong got off his mat and asked,
小子敢問回東之齊夫子有憂色何邪
"May I be so bold as to inquire why the Master has such a worried expression now that Hui has gone east to Qi?"

***

孔子曰善哉汝問
"Excellent---this question of yours," said Confucius.

昔者管子有言丘甚善之曰
"Guanzi[^18-6] had a saying that I much approve of:
褚小者不可以懷大
'Small bags won't hold big things;
綆短者不可以汲深
short well ropes won't dip up deep water.'

[^18-6]: Guan Zhong, a seventh-century statesman of Qi whom Confucius, judging from the _Analects_, admired.

夫若是者以為命有所成
In the same way I believe that fate has certain forms,
而形有所適也
and the body, certain appropriate uses.

夫不可損益
You can't add to or take away from these.

吾恐回與齊侯
I'm afraid that when Hui gets to Qi,
言堯舜黃帝之道
he will start telling the marquis of Qi about the ways of Yao, Shun, and the Yellow Emperor
而重以燧人神農之言
and then will go on to speak about Suiren and Shennong.[^18-7]

[^18-7]: Suiren and Shennong are mythical culture heroes, the discoverers of fire and agriculture, respectively.

彼將內求於己而不得
The marquis will then look for similar greatness within himself and fail to find it.

不得則惑
Failing to find it, he will become distraught,
人惑則死
and when a man becomes distraught, he kills.

***

且女獨不聞邪
"Haven't you heard this story?

昔者海鳥止於魯郊
Once a sea bird alighted in the suburbs of the Lu capital.

魯侯御而觴之於廟
The marquis of Lu escorted it to the ancestral temple, where he entertained it,
奏九韶以為樂
performing the Nine Shao music for it to listen to
具太牢以為善
and presenting it with the meat of the Tailao sacrifice to feast on.

鳥乃眩視憂悲
But the bird only looked dazed and forlorn,
不敢食一臠
refusing to eat a single slice of meat
不敢飲一杯
or drink a cup of wine,
三日而死
and in three days it was dead.

此以己養養鳥也
This is to try to nourish a bird with what would nourish you
非以鳥養養鳥也
instead of what would nourish a bird.

夫以鳥養養鳥者
If you want to nourish a bird with what nourishes a bird,
宜栖之深林
then you should let it roost in the deep forest,
遊之壇陸
play among the banks and islands,
浮之江湖
float on the rivers and lakes,
食之鰍鰷
eat mudfish and minnows,
隨行列而止
follow the rest of the flock in flight and rest,
委蛇而處
and live in any way it chooses.

彼唯人言之惡聞
A bird hates to hear even the sound of human voices,
奚以夫譊譊為乎
much less all that hubbub and to-do.

咸池九韶之樂
Try performing the Xianchi and Nine Shao music
張之洞庭之野
in the wilds around Lake Dongting---
鳥聞之而飛
when the birds hear it, they will fly off;
獸聞之而走
when the animals hear it, they will run away;
魚聞之而下入
when the fish hear it, they will dive to the bottom.

人卒聞之
Only the people who hear it
相與還而觀之
will gather around to listen.

魚處水而生
Fish live in water and thrive,
人處水而死
but if men tried to live in water, they would die.

故必相與異其好惡故異也
Creatures differ because they have different likes and dislikes.

故先聖不一其能
Therefore the former sages never required the same ability from all creatures
不同其事
or made them all do the same thing.

名止於實
Names should stop when they have expressed reality,
義設於適
concepts of right should be founded on what is suitable.

是之謂條達而福持
This is what it means to have command of reason and good fortune to support you."

### Liezi questions a hundred-year-old skull

列子行食於道
Liezi was on a trip and was eating by the roadside
從見百歲髑髏
when he saw a hundred-year-old skull.

攓蓬而指之曰
Pulling away the weeds and pointing his finger, he said,
唯予與汝知而未嘗死
"Only you and I know that you have never died
未嘗生也
and you have never lived.

若果養乎
Are you really unhappy?[^18-8]

[^18-8]: Following Yu Yue's interpretation.

予果歡乎
Am I really enjoying myself?"

### The seeds of things

種有幾
The seeds of things have mysterious workings.

得水
In the water,
則為㡭
they become Break Vine;
得水土之際
on the edges of the water,
則為蛙蠙之衣
they become Frog's Robe.
生於陵屯
If they sprout on the slopes,
則為陵舄
they become Hill Slippers.
陵舄得鬱棲
If Hill Slippers get rich soil,
則為烏足
they turn into Crow's Feet.

烏足之根為蠐螬
The roots of Crow's Feet turn into maggots,
其葉為蝴蝶
and their leaves turn into butterflies.

胡蝶胥也化
Before long, the butterflies are transformed
而為蟲生於灶下
and turn into insects that live under the stove;
其狀若脫
they look like snakes,
其名為鴝掇
and their name is Qutuo.

鴝掇千日為鳥
After a thousand days, the Qutuo insects become birds
其名曰乾餘骨
called Dried Leftover Bones.

乾餘骨之沬為斯彌
The saliva of the Dried Leftover Bones becomes Simi bugs,
斯彌為食醯
and the Simi bugs become Vinegar Eaters.

頤輅生乎食醯
Yiluo bugs are born from the Vinegar Eaters,
黃軦生乎九猷
and Huangshuang bugs, from Jiuyou bugs.

(九猷生乎瞀芮)
Jiuyou bugs are born from Mourui bugs,
瞀芮生乎腐蠸[^rse-18-1]
and Mourui bugs are born from Rot Grubs,
(腐蠸生乎羊奚)
and Rot Grubs are born from Sheep's Groom.

[^rse-18-1]: The phrases before and after this phrase
are missing from ctext, but present in Liu, and translated by Watson. --RSE

羊奚比乎不筍久竹生青寧
Sheep's Groom couples with bamboo that has not sprouted for a long while and produces Green Peace plants.

青寧生程
Green Peace plants produce leopards,
程生馬
and leopards produce horses,
馬生人
and horses produce men.

人又反入於機
Men in time return again to the mysterious workings.

萬物皆出於機
So all creatures come out of the mysterious workings
皆入於機
and go back into them again.[^18-9]

[^18-9]: The text of this paragraph, a romp through ancient Chinese nature lore, is doubtful at many points.
