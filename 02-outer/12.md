## 12 天地 Heaven and Earth

天地雖大
其化均也
Heaven and earth are huge,
but they are alike in their transformations.

萬物雖多
其治一也
The ten thousand things are numerous,
but they are one in their good order.

人卒雖眾
其主君也
Human beings are many,
but they all are subjects of the sovereign.

君原於德
而成於天
The sovereign finds his source in Virtue,
his completion in Heaven.

故曰玄古之君天下無為也
天德而已矣
Therefore it is said that the sovereign of dark antiquity ruled the world through inaction,
through Heavenly Virtue and nothing more.

***

以道觀言
Look at words in the light of the Way---
而天下之君正
then the sovereign of the world will be upright.[^12-1]

[^12-1]: Perhaps a reference to the Confucian doctrine of the rectification of names,
that is, the necessity to make certain that the one who is called "ruler" is in fact a true ruler, etc.
The writer of this chapter seems to be attempting to effect a compromise
between Daoist and Confucian ideals of government.

以道觀分
Look at distinctions in the light of the Way---
而君臣之義明
then the duty[^12-2] of sovereign and subject will be clear.

[^12-2]: *Yi*, elsewhere translated as "righteousness."

以道觀能
Look at abilities in the light of the Way---
而天下之官治
then the officials of the world will be well ordered.

以道汎觀
Look everywhere in the light of the Way---
而萬物之應備
then the response of the ten thousand things will be complete.

***

故通於天地者德道也
Pervading Heaven and earth: that is the Way.[^12-3]

[^12-3]: As pointed out by commentators, the position of the *de* and that of the *dao*
in the next sentence should be reversed to match the order of the sorites that follows.
But the text is probably faulty.

行於萬物者德也
Moving among the ten thousand things: that is Virtue.

上治人者事也
Superiors governing the men below them: that is called administration.

能有所藝者技也
Ability finding trained expression: that is called skill.

技兼於事
事兼於義
義兼於德
德兼於道
道兼於天
Skill is subsumed in administration,
administration in duty,
duty in Virtue,
Virtue in the Way,
and the Way in Heaven.

故曰古之畜天下者無欲
而天下足
無為
而萬物化
Therefore it is said, those who shepherded the world in ancient times were without desire,
and the world was satisfied,
without action,
and the ten thousand things were transformed.

淵靜
而百姓定
They were deep and silent,
and the hundred clans were at rest.

記曰通於一
而萬事畢
The Record says: "Stick to the One,
and the ten thousand tasks will be accomplished;
無心得
而鬼神服
achieve mindlessness,
and the gods and spirits will bow down."[^12-4]

[^12-4]: It is not known what "Record" the writer is quoting.

***

夫子曰夫道覆載萬物者也
The Master said:[^12-5] The Way covers and bears up the ten thousand things---
洋洋乎大哉
vast, vast is its greatness!

[^12-5]: The Master has been variously identified as Laozi, Zhuangzi, or Confucius.

君子不可以不刳心焉
The gentleman must pluck out his mind!

無為為之之謂天
To act through inaction is called Heaven.

無為言之之謂德
To speak through inaction is called Virtue.

愛人利物之謂仁
To love men and bring profit to things is called benevolence.

不同同之之謂大
To make the unlike alike is called magnitude.

行不崖異之謂寬
To move beyond barrier and distinction is called liberality.

有萬不同之謂富
To possess the ten thousand unlikes is called wealth.

故執德之謂紀
To hold fast to Virtue is called enrootment.

德成之謂立
To mature in Virtue is called establishment.

循於道之謂備
To follow the Way is called completion.

不以物挫志之謂完
To see that external things do not blunt the will is called perfection.

君子明於此十者
則韜乎其事心之大也
沛乎其為萬物逝也
When the gentleman clearly comprehends these ten things,
then how huge will be the greatness of his mind setting forth,
how endless his ramblings with the ten thousand things!

***

若然者藏金於山
藏珠於淵
Such a man will leave the gold hidden in the mountains,
the pearls hidden in the depths.

不利貨財
不近貴富
不樂壽
不哀夭
不榮通
不醜窮
He will see no profit in money and goods,
no enticement in eminence and wealth,
no joy in long life,
no grief in early death,
no honor in affluence,
no shame in poverty.

壽夭俱忘
窮通不足言矣
(Long life and early death [accompany each other to oblivion],
affluence and poverty [are an insufficient measure of worth].)

不拘一世之利以為己私分
He will not snatch the profits of a whole generation and make them his private hoard;
不以王天下為己處顯
he will not lord it over the world and think that he dwells in glory.

顯則明
萬物一府
死生同狀
His glory is enlightenment,
[for he knows that] the ten thousand things belong to one storehouse,
that life and death share the same body.

***

夫子曰夫道
淵乎其居也
漻乎其清也
The Master said: The Way---
how deep its dwelling,
how pure its clearness!

金石不得無以鳴
Without it, the bells and chiming stones will not sound.

故金石有聲
不考不鳴
The bells and stones have voices,
but unless they are struck,
they will not sound.

萬物孰能定之
The ten thousand things---
who can make them be still?

***

夫王德之人
素逝而恥通於事
The man of kingly Virtue
moves in simplicity and is ashamed to be a master of facts.

立之本原
而知通於神
He takes his stand in the original source,
and his understanding extends to the spirits.

故其德廣
Therefore his Virtue is far-reaching.

其心之出有物採之
His mind moves forth only when some external thing has roused it.

故形非道不生
生非德不明
Without the Way, the body can have no life,
and without Virtue, life can have no clarity.

存形窮生
立德明道
To preserve the body and live out life,
to establish Virtue and make clear the Way---
非王德者邪
is this not kingly Virtue?

蕩蕩乎
忽然出
勃然動
而萬物從之乎
Broad and boundless,
suddenly he emerges,
abruptly he moves,
and the ten thousand things follow him---
此謂王德之人
this is what is called the man of kingly Virtue!

***

視乎冥冥
聽乎無聲
He sees in the darkest dark,
hears where there is no sound.

冥冥之中獨見曉焉
In the midst of darkness, he alone sees the dawn;
無聲之中獨聞和焉
in the midst of the soundless, he alone hears harmony.

故深之又深而能物焉
Therefore, in depth piled upon depth, he can spy out the thing;
神之又神而能精焉
in spirituality piled upon spirituality, he can discover the essence.[^12-6]

[^12-6]: Compare _Daodejing_ XXI: "shadowy and indistinct, within it is a thing;
dim and dark, within it is an essence."

故其與萬物接也
至無而供其求
So in his dealings with the ten thousand things,
he supplies all their wants out of total nothingness.

時騁而要其宿
大小
長短
修遠
Racing with the hour, he seeks lodging for a night,
in the great, the small,
the long, the short,
the near, the far.[^12-7]

[^12-7]: That is, he accommodates himself to external phenomena
as a traveler accommodates himself to the conditions of the journey.
In the main, I follow Fukunaga's interpretation, though the sentence is very obscure.

---

黃帝遊乎赤水之北
登乎崑崙之丘而南望
The Yellow Emperor went wandering north of the Red Water,
ascended the slopes of Kunlun, and gazed south.

還歸
遺其玄珠
When he got home,
he discovered he had lost his Dark Pearl.

使知索之
而不得
He sent Knowledge to look for it,
but Knowledge couldn't find it.

使離朱索之
而不得
He sent the keen-eyed Li Zhu to look for it,
but Li Zhu couldn't find it.

使喫詬索之
而不得也
He sent Wrangling Debate to look for it,
but Wrangling Debate couldn't find it.

乃使象罔
象罔得之
At last he tried employing Shapeless,
and Shapeless found it.

***

黃帝曰異哉
The Yellow Emperor said, "How odd!---
象罔乃可以得之乎
in the end it was Shapeless who was able to find it!"

---

堯之師曰許由
Yao's teacher was Xu You;
許由之師曰齧缺
Xu You's teacher was Nie Que;
齧缺之師曰王倪
Nie Que's teacher was Wang Ni;
王倪之師曰被衣
and Wang Ni's teacher was Piyi.

堯問於許由曰齧缺可以配天乎
Yao asked Xu You, "Would Nie Que do as the counterpart of Heaven?

吾藉王倪以要之
I could get Wang Ni to ask him to take over the throne from me."

***

許由曰殆哉
Xu You said, "Watch out!

圾乎天下
You'll put the world in danger!

齧缺之為人也聰明叡知
給數以敏
Nie Que is a man of keen intelligence and superb understanding,
nimble-witted and sharp.

其性過人
而又乃以人受天
His inborn nature surpasses that of other men,
and he knows how to exploit what Heaven has given him through human devices.

彼審乎禁過
而不知過之所由生
He would do his best to prevent error,
but he doesn't understand the source from which error arises.

與之配天乎
Make *him* the counterpart of Heaven?

彼且乘人而無天
Watch---he will start leaning on men and forget about Heaven.

方且本身而異形
He will put himself first and relegate others to a class apart.

方且尊知而火馳
He will worship knowledge and chase after it with the speed of fire.

方且為緒使
方且為物絯
方且四顧而物應
方且應眾宜
方且與物化
而未始有恒
He will become the servant of causes,
the victim of things,
looking in all four directions to see how things are faring,
trying to attend to all wants,
changing along with things,
and possessing no trace of any constancy of his own.

夫何足以配天乎
How could he possibly do as counterpart of Heaven?

雖然有族有祖
However, there are clans, and there are clan heads.

可以為眾父
而不可以為眾父父
He might do as the father of one branch,
though he would never do as the father of the father of the branch.

治亂之率也
北面之禍也
南面之賊也
His kind are the forerunners of disorder,
a disaster to the ministers facing north,
a peril to the sovereign facing south!"

---

堯觀乎華
華封人曰嘻
Yao was seeing the sights at Hua
when the border guard of Hua said, "Aha---
聖人
a sage!

請祝聖人
I beg to offer up prayers for the sage.

使聖人壽
They will bring the sage long life!"

***

堯曰辭
Yao said, "No, thanks."

使聖人富
"They will bring the sage riches!"

***

堯曰辭
Yao said, "No, thanks."

使聖人多男子
"They will bring the sage many sons!"

***

堯曰辭
Yao said, "No, thanks."

***

封人曰壽富多男子
"Long life, riches, many sons---
人之所欲也
these are what all men desire!" said the border guard.

女獨不欲何邪
"How is it that you alone do not desire them?"

***

堯曰多男子則多懼
Yao said, "Many sons mean many fears.

富則多事
Riches mean many troubles.

壽則多辱
Long life means many shames.

是三者非所以養德也
These three are of no use in nourishing Virtue---
故辭
therefore I decline them."

***

封人曰始也我以女為聖人邪
The border guard said, "At first I took you for a sage.

今然君子也
Now I see you are a mere gentleman.

天生萬民
必授之職
When Heaven gives birth to the ten thousand people,
it is certain to have jobs to assign to them.

多男子而授之職
則何懼之有
If you have many sons and their jobs are assigned to them,
what is there to fear?

富而使人分之
則何事之有
If you share your riches with other men,
what troubles will you have?

夫聖人鶉
居而鷇食鳥
行而無彰
The true sage is a quail at rest,
a little fledgling at its meal,
a bird in flight that leaves no trail behind.

天下有道
則與物皆昌
When the world has the Way,
he joins in the chorus with all other things.

天下無道
則修德就閒
When the world is without the Way,
he nurses his Virtue and retires in leisure.

千歲厭世
去而上僊
乘彼白雲
至於帝鄉
And after a thousand years, should he tire of the world,
he will leave it and ascend to the immortals,
riding on those white clouds
all the way up to the village of God.

三患莫至
The three worries you have cited never touch him;
身常無殃
his body is forever free of peril.

則何辱之有
How can he suffer any shame?"

***

封人去之
The border guard turned and left.

堯隨之曰請
Yao followed him, saying, "Please---
問
I would like to ask you ..."

封人曰退已
"Go away!" said the border guard.

---

堯治天下
伯成子高立為諸侯
When Yao ruled the world,
Bocheng Zigao was enfeoffed as one of his noblemen.

堯授舜
舜授禹
伯成子高辭為諸侯而耕
But when Yao passed the throne to Shun,
and Shun passed it to Yu,
Bocheng Zigao relinquished his title and took up farming.

禹往見之則耕在野
Yu went to see him and found him working in the fields.

禹趨就下風
立而問焉曰昔堯治天下
吾子立為諸侯
Yu scurried forward in the humblest manner,
came to a halt, and said, "In former times when Yao ruled the world,
sir, you served as one of his noblemen.

堯授舜
舜授予
而吾子辭為諸侯而耕
But when Yao passed the throne to Shun,
and Shun passed it to me,
you relinquished your title and took up farming.

敢問其故何也
May I be so bold as to ask why?"

***

子高曰昔堯治天下
不賞而民勸
Zigao said, "In former times when Yao ruled the world,
he handed out no rewards, and yet the people worked hard;
不罰而民畏
he handed out no punishments, and yet the people were cautious.

今子賞罰
而民且不仁
Now you reward and punish,
and still the people fail to do good.

德自此衰
From now on, Virtue will decay;
刑自此立
from now on, penalties will prevail.

後世之亂自此始矣
The disorder of future ages will have its beginning here!

夫子闔行邪
You had better be on your way now---
無落吾事
don't interrupt my work!"

俋俋乎耕
而不顧
Busily, busily he proceeded with his farm work,
never turning to look back.

---

泰初有無
In the Great Beginning, there was nonbeing;
無有無名
there was no being, no name.

一之所起
Out of it arose One;
有一而未形
there was One, but it had no form.

物得以生
謂之德
Things got hold of it and it came to life,
and it was called Virtue.

未形者有分
Before things had forms, they had their allotments;
且然無間謂之命
these were of many kinds but not cut off from one another, and they were called fates.

留動而生物
物成生理
Out of the flow and flux, things were born,
and as they grew, they developed distinctive shapes;
謂之形
these were called forms.

形體保神
各有儀則
謂之性
The forms and bodies held within them spirits,
each with its own characteristics and limitations,
and this was called the inborn nature.

性修反德
德至同於初
If the nature is trained, you may return to Virtue,
and Virtue at its highest peak is identical with the Beginning.

同乃虛
Being identical, you will be empty;
虛乃大
being empty, you will be great.

合喙鳴
喙鳴合
與天地為合
You may join in the cheeping and chirping,
and when you have joined in the cheeping and chirping,
you may join with Heaven and earth.

其合緡緡
若愚若昏
Your joining is wild and confused,
as though you were stupid, as though you were demented.

是謂玄德
This is called Dark Virtue.

同乎大順
Rude and unwitting, you take part in the Great Submission.

---

夫子問於老聃曰有人治道
若相放
可不可然不然
Confucius said to Lao Dan, "Here's a man who works to master the Way
as though he were trying to talk down an opponent,[^12-8]
making the unacceptable acceptable, the not so, so.

[^12-8]: Following Ma Xulun, I read *bang* (slander) in place of *fang*.

辯者有言曰離堅白
若縣宇若是
As the rhetoricians say, he can separate 'hard' from 'white'
as clearly as though they were dangling from the eaves there.

則可謂聖人乎
Can a man like this be called a sage?"

***

老聃曰是胥易
技係
勞形
怵心者也
Lao Dan said, "A man like this is a drudging slave,
a craftsman bound to his calling,
wearing out his body,
grieving his mind.

執留之狗成思
Because the dog can catch rats, he ends up on a leash.[^12-9]

[^12-9]: Following Sun Yirang, I read *lei* in place of *si*;
compare the parallel passage on p. 56.

猿狙之便自山林來
Because of his nimbleness, the monkey is dragged down from the mountain forest.

丘予告若
Qiu,[^12-10] I'm going to tell you something---
而所不能聞與
而所不能言
something you could never hear for yourself
and something you would never know how to speak of.

[^12-10]: Confucius's familiar name. In using it to address Confucius face to face,
Laozi is expressing great familiarity and/or contempt.

凡有首有趾無心無耳者
People who have heads and feet but no minds and no ears---
眾
there are mobs of them.

有形者與無形無狀而皆存者
To think that beings with bodies can all go on existing along with that which is bodiless and formless---
盡無
it can never happen!

其動止也
其死生也
其廢起也
A man's stops and starts,
his life and death,
his rises and falls---
此又非其所以也
none of these can he do anything about.

有治在人
Yet he thinks that the mastery of them lies with man!

忘乎物
忘乎天
其名為忘己
Forget things,
forget Heaven,
and be called a forgetter of self.

忘己之人
是之謂入於天
The man who has forgotten self
may be said to have entered Heaven."

將閭葂見季徹曰魯君謂葂也
Jianglü Mian went to see Ji Che and said, "The ruler of Lu begged me to give him some instruction.

曰請受教
辭不獲命
既已告矣
I declined,
but he wouldn't let me go,
and so I had no choice but to tell him something.

未知中否
請嘗薦之
I don't know whether or not what I said was right,
but I would like to try repeating it to you.

吾謂魯君曰必服恭儉
I said to the ruler of Lu, 'You must be courteous and temperate!

拔出公忠之屬
而無阿私
民孰敢不輯
Pick out and promote those who are loyal and public-spirited,
allow no flattery or favoritism,
and then who of your people will venture to be unruly?'"

***

季徹局局然笑
Ji Che heehawed with laughter.

曰若夫子之言於帝王之德
猶螳蜋之怒臂以當車軼
"As far as the Virtue of emperors and kings is concerned," he said,
"your advice is like the praying mantis that waved its arms angrily in front of an approaching carriage---
則必不勝任矣
it just isn't up to the job.

且若是
則其自為處危
其觀臺多物
If the ruler of Lu went about it in that way,
he would simply get himself all stirred up,[^12-11]
place himself on a tower or a terrace.

[^12-11]: Following texts that read *ju* (agitated) in place of *chu*.

將往投
跡者眾
Then things would flock around him,
and the crowd would turn its steps in his direction!"

***

將閭葂覤覤然驚
Jianglü Mian's eyes bugged out in amazement.

曰葂也汒若於夫子之所言矣
"I am dumbfounded by your words," he said.

雖然願先生之言其風也
"Nevertheless, I would like to hear how the Master would speak on this subject."

***

季徹曰大聖之治天下也
搖蕩民心使之成教易俗
Ji Che said, "When a great sage rules the world,
he makes the minds of his people free and far wandering.

舉滅其賊心而皆進其獨志
On this basis, he fashions teachings and simplifies customs,
wiping out all treason from their minds and allowing each to pursue his own will.

若性之自為
而民不知其所由然
All is done in accordance with the inborn nature,
and yet the people do not know why it is like this.

若然者
豈兄堯舜之教民
溟滓然弟之哉
Proceeding in this way,
what need has he either to revere the way in which Yao and Shun taught their people
or to look down on it in lofty contempt?

欲同乎德而心居矣
His only desire is for unity with Virtue and the repose of the mind."

子貢南遊於楚
反於晉
過漢陰
見一丈人方將為圃畦
Zigong traveled south to Chu,
and on his way back through Jin,
as he passed along the south bank of the Han,
he saw an old man preparing his fields for planting.

鑿隧而入井
抱甕而出灌
He had hollowed out an opening by which he entered the well
and from which he emerged, lugging a pitcher, which he carried out to water the fields.

搰搰然用力甚多而見功寡
Grunting and puffing, he used up a great deal of energy and produced very little result.

***

子貢曰有械於此
"There is a machine for this sort of thing," said Zigong.

一日浸百畦
用力甚寡而見功多
"In one day it can water a hundred fields,
demanding very little effort and producing excellent results.

夫子不欲乎
Wouldn't you like one?"

***

為圃者卬而視之
The gardener raised his head and looked at Zigong.

曰奈何
"How does it work?"

***

曰鑿木為機
"It's a contraption made by shaping a piece of wood.

後重前輕
挈水若抽
數如泆湯
The back end is heavy and the front end light
and it raises the water as though it were pouring it out,
so fast that it seems to boil right over!

其名為槔
It's called a well sweep."

***

為圃者忿然作色而笑曰吾聞之吾師
有機械者必有機事
The gardener flushed with anger and then said with a laugh, "I've heard my teacher say,
where there are machines, there are bound to be machine worries;
有機事者必有機心
where there are machine worries, there are bound to be machine hearts.

機心存於胸中則純白不備
純白不備則神生不定
With a machine heart in your breast, you've spoiled what was pure and simple,
and without the pure and simple, the life of the spirit knows no rest.

神生不定者
道之所不載也
Where the life of the spirit knows no rest,
the Way will cease to buoy you up.

吾非不知
It's not that I don't know about your machine---
羞而不為也
I would be ashamed to use it!"

***

子貢瞞然慙
俯而不對
Zigong blushed with chagrin,
looked down, and made no reply.

有間為圃者曰子奚為者邪
After a while, the gardener said, "Who are you, anyway?"

曰孔丘之徒也
"A disciple of Kong Qiu."[^12-12]

[^12-12]: Confucius.

***

為圃者曰子非夫博學以擬聖
於于以蓋眾
獨弦哀歌以賣名聲於天下者乎
"Oh---then you must be one of those who broaden their learning in order to ape the sages,
heaping absurd nonsense on the crowd,
plucking the strings and singing sad songs all by yourself in hopes of buying fame in the world!

汝方將忘汝神氣
墮汝形骸
You would do best to forget your spirit and breath,
break up your body and limbs---
而庶幾乎
then you might be able to get somewhere.

而身之不能治
You don't even know how to look after your own body---
而何暇治天下乎
how do you have any time to think about looking after the world!

子往矣
On your way now!

無乏吾事
Don't interfere with my work!"

***

子貢卑陬失色
Zigong frowned, and the color drained from his face.

頊頊然不自得
行三十里而後愈
Dazed and rattled, he couldn't seem to pull himself together,
and it was only after he had walked on for some thirty *li* that he began to recover.

***

其弟子曰向之人何為者邪
One of his disciples said, "Who was that man just now?

夫子何故見之變容失色
終日不自反邪
Why did you change your expression and lose your color like that, Master,
so that it took you all day to get back to normal?"

***

曰始以為天下一人耳
"I used to think there was only one real man in the world," said Zigong.

不知復有夫人也
"I didn't know that there was this other one.

吾聞之夫子
事求可
功求成
I have heard Confucius say that
in affairs you aim for what is right,
and in undertakings you aim for success.

用力少
見功多者
To spend little effort
and achieve big results---
聖人之道
that is the Way of the sage.

今徒不然
Now it seems that this isn't so.

執道者德全
He who holds fast to the Way is complete in Virtue;
德全者形全
being complete in Virtue, he is complete in body;
形全者神全
being complete in body, he is complete in spirit;
神全者
聖人之道也
and to be complete in spirit
is the Way of the sage.

託生與民
並行
而不知其所之
He is content to live among the people,
to walk by their side,
and never know where he is going.

汒乎淳備哉
Witless, his purity is complete.

功利
機巧
Achievement, profit,
machines, skill---
必忘夫人之心
they have no place in this man's mind!

若夫人者非其志不之
非其心不為
A man like this will not go where he has no will to go,
will not do what he has no mind to do.

雖以天下譽之得其所謂
謷然不顧
Though the world might praise him and say he had really found something,
he would look unconcerned and never turn his head;
以天下非之失其所謂
儻然不受
though the world might condemn him and say he had lost something,
he would look serene and pay no heed.

天下之非譽
無益損焉
The praise and blame of the world
are no loss or gain to him.

是謂全德之人哉
He may be called a man of Complete Virtue.

我之謂風波之民
I---I am a man of the wind-blown waves."

***

反於魯
以告孔子
When Zigong got back to Lu,
he reported the incident to Confucius.

孔子曰彼假修渾沌氏之術者也
Confucius said, "He is one of those bogus practitioners of the arts of Mr. Chaos.[^12-13]

[^12-13]: On Mr. Chaos (Hundun), see p. 59.

識其一
不知其二
He knows the first thing
but doesn't understand the second.

治其內
而不治其外
He looks after what is on the inside
but doesn't look after what is on the outside.

夫明白入素
無為復朴
體性抱神
以遊世俗之間者
A man of true brightness and purity who can enter into simplicity,
who can return to the primitive through inaction,
give body to his inborn nature, and embrace his spirit,
and in this way wander through the everyday world---
汝將固驚邪
if you had met one like that, you would have had real cause for astonishment.[^12-14]

[^12-14]: That is, the true man of the Way does not retire from the world or reject society and its inventions.

且渾沌氏之術
予與汝何足以識之哉
As for the arts of Mr. Chaos,
you and I need not bother to find out about them."

---

諄芒將東之大壑
適遇苑風於東海之濱
Zhun Mang was on his way east to the Great Valley of the sea
when he happened to meet Yuan Feng by the shore of the eastern ocean.[^12-15]

[^12-15]: The names of the persons in the anecdote are allegorical,
Zhun Mang meaning something like "Artless and Forgetful" and Yuan Feng meaning "Little Wind."

苑風曰子將奚之
Yuan Feng said, "Where are you going?"

曰將之大壑
"I'm going to the Great Valley."

曰奚為焉
"What will you do there?"

***

曰夫大壑之為物也
注焉而不滿
酌焉而不竭
"The Great Valley is the sort of thing
you can pour into and it never gets full,
dip from and it never runs dry.

吾將遊焉
I'm going to wander there."

***

苑風曰夫子無意於橫目之民乎
Yuan Feng said, "Don't you care about what happens to ordinary men?

願聞聖治
Please, won't you tell me about the government of the sage?"

***

諄芒曰聖治乎
"The government of the sage?" said Zhun Mang.

官施而不失其宜
"Assign offices so that no abilities are overlooked;
拔舉而不失其能
promote men so that no talents are neglected.

畢見其情事
而行其所為
Always know the true facts,
and let men do what they are best at.

行言自為而天下化
手撓顧指
四方之民莫不俱至
When actions and words proceed properly and the world is transformed,
then at a wave of the hand or a tilt of the chin,
all the people of the four directions will come flocking to you.

此之謂聖治
This is called the government of the sage."

---

願聞德人
"May I ask about the man of Virtue?"

***

曰德人者居無思行無慮
"The man of Virtue rests without thought, moves without plan.

不藏是非美惡
He has no use for right and wrong, beautiful and ugly.

四海之內共利之之謂悅
共給之之謂安
To share profit with all things within the four seas is his happiness,
to look after their needs is his peace.

怊乎若嬰兒之失其母也
Sad faced, he's like a little child who has lost his mother.

儻乎若行而失其道也
Bewildered, he's like a traveler who has lost his way.

財用有餘
而不知其所自來
He has more than enough wealth and goods,
but he doesn't know where they come from.

飲食取足
而不知其所從
He gets all he needs to eat and drink,
but he doesn't know how he gets it.

此謂德人之容
This is called the manner of the man of Virtue."

願聞神人
"May I ask about the man of spirit?"

***

曰上神乘光
"He lets his spirit ascend and mount on the light;
與形滅亡
with his bodily form, he dissolves and is gone.

此謂照曠
This is called the Illumination of Vastness.

天地樂而萬事銷亡
He lives out his fate, follows to the end his true form,
and rests in the joy of Heaven and earth while the ten thousand cares melt away.

萬物復情
So all things return to their true form.

此之謂混冥
This is called Muddled Darkness."

---

門無鬼與赤張滿稽
觀於武王之師
Men Wugui and Chizhang Manqui
were watching the troops of King Wu.[^12-16]

[^12-16]: If they were viewing the actual troops, the episode must be set in the eleventh century BCE,
when King Wu of the Zhou attacked and overthrew the last ruler of the Shang dynasty.
But perhaps they were watching the court dances performed in later ages that reenacted the campaign.
The "man of the Yu clan" in the following sentence is the sage ruler
who did not have to launch any military expeditions.

赤張滿稽曰不及有虞氏乎
Chizhang Manqui said, "He is no match for the man of the Yu clan.

故離此患也
That's why he runs into all this trouble!"

***

門無鬼曰天下均治而有虞氏治之邪
Men Wugui said, "Was the world already in good order when the man of the Yu clan came along to order it?

其亂而後治之與
Or was it in disorder, and later he brought it in order?"

***

赤張滿稽曰天下均治之為願
Chizhang Manqui said, "Everybody wants to see the world well ordered.

而何計以有虞氏為
If it had been so already, what point would there have been in calling on the man of the Yu clan?

有虞氏之藥瘍也
The man of the Yu clan was medicine to a sore.

禿而施髢
病而求醫
孝子操藥以修慈父
其色燋然
But to wait until you go bald and then buy a wig,
to wait until you get sick and then call for a doctor,
to prepare the medicine like a true filial son and present it to your loving father,
wearing a grim and haggard look---
聖人羞之
this the true sage would be ashamed to do.

至德之世不尚賢
In an age of Perfect Virtue, the worthy are not honored;
不使能
the talented are not employed.

上如標枝
Rulers are like the high branches of a tree;
民如野鹿
the people, like the deer of the fields.

端正而不知以為義
They do what is right, but they do not know that this is righteousness.

相愛而不知以為仁
They love one another, but they do not know that this is benevolence.

實而不知以為忠
They are truehearted but do not know that this is loyalty.

當而不知以為信
They are trustworthy but do not know that this is good faith.

蠢動而相使
不以為賜
They wriggle around like insects, performing services for one another,
but do not know that they are being kind.

是故行而無迹
事而無傳
Therefore they move without leaving any trail behind,
act without leaving any memory of their deeds."

---

孝子不諛其親
忠臣不諂其君
臣子之盛也
When a filial son does not fawn on his parents,
when a loyal minister does not flatter his lord,
they are the finest of sons and ministers.

親之所言而然
所行而善
則世俗謂之不肖子
He who agrees with everything his parents say
and approves of everything they do
is regarded by popular opinion as an unworthy son;
君之所言而然
所行而善
則世俗謂之不肖臣
he who agrees with everything his lord says
and approves of everything his lord does
is regarded by popular opinion as an unworthy minister.[^12-17]

[^12-17]: Because it is the duty of the son and minister to reprimand his parents and lord,
respectively, when they are clearly in the wrong.

而未知此其必然邪
But in other cases, men do not realize that the same principle should apply.

世俗之所謂然而然之
所謂善而善之
則不謂之道諛之人也
If a man agrees with everything that popular opinion says
and regards as good everything that popular opinion regards as good,
he is not, as you might expect, called a sycophant and a flatterer.

然則俗固嚴於親
而尊於君邪
Are we to assume, then, that popular opinion commands more authority than one's parents
or is more to be honored than one's lord?

***

謂己道人則勃然作色
Call a man a sycophant, and he flushes with anger;
謂己諛人則怫然作色
call him a flatterer, and he turns crimson with rage.

而終身道人也
Yet all his life, he will continue to be a sycophant;
終身諛人也
all his life, he will continue to be a flatterer.

合譬飾辭聚眾也
是始終
本末不相坐
See him set forth his analogies and polish his fine phrases to draw a crowd,
until the beginning and end,
the root and branches of his argument no longer match![^12-18]

[^12-18]: Following texts that omit the *zui* and adopting Chu Boxiu's interpretation;
the reference is apparently to the rhetoricians.

垂衣裳
設采色
動容貌
以媚一世
See him spread out his robes,
display his bright colors,
put on a solemn face
in hopes of currying favor with the age---
而不自謂道諛
and yet he does not recognize himself as a sycophant or a flatterer.

與夫人之為徒
通是非
而不自謂眾人
See him with his followers
laying down the law on right and wrong,
and yet he does not recognize himself as one of the mob.

愚之至也
This is the height of foolishness!

***

知其愚者非大愚也
He who knows he is a fool is not the biggest fool;
知其惑者非大惑也
he who knows he is confused is not in the worst confusion.

大惑者終身不解
The man in the worst confusion will end his life without ever getting straightened out;
大愚者終身不靈
the biggest fool will end his life without ever seeing the light.

三人行而一人惑
所適者猶可致也
If three men are traveling along and one is confused,
they will still get where they are going---
惑者少也
because confusion is in the minority.

二人惑
則勞而不至
But if two of them are confused,
then they can walk until they are exhausted and never get anywhere---
惑者勝也
because confusion is in the majority.

而今也以天下惑
予雖有祈嚮
不可得也
And with all the confusion in the world these days,
no matter how often I point the way,
it does no good.

不亦悲乎
Sad, is it not?

***

大聲不入於里耳
折楊
皇荂
則嗑然而笑
Great music is lost on the ears of the villagers,
but play them "The Breaking of the Willow"
or "Bright Flowers,"
and they grin from ear to ear.

是故高言不止於眾人之心
In the same way, lofty words make no impression on the minds of the mob.

至言不出
俗言勝也
Superior words gain no hearing
because vulgar words are in the majority.

以二缶鍾惑
而所適不得矣
It is like the case of the two travelers tramping along in confusion
and never getting where they are going.[^12-19]

[^12-19]: Following Lu Deming's emendations.

而今也以天下惑
予雖有祈嚮
其庸可得邪
With all the confusion in the world these days,
no matter how often I point the way,
what good does it do?

知其不可得也而強之
又一惑也
And if I know it does no good and still make myself do it,
this too is a kind of confusion.

故莫若釋之而不推
So it is best to leave things alone and not force them.

不推
誰其比憂
If I don't force things,
at least I won't cause anyone any worry.

***

厲之人夜半生其子
遽取火而視之
汲汲然惟恐其似己也
When the leper woman gives birth to a child in the dead of the night,
she rushes to fetch a torch and examine it,
trembling with terror lest it look like herself.[^12-20]

[^12-20]: Is this sentence intended to belong with what precedes it or with what follows it?
I am unable to tell.

---

百年之木
破為犧尊
青黃而文之
其斷在溝中
The hundred-year-old tree
is hacked up to make bowls for the sacrificial wine,
blue and yellow with patterns on them,
and the chips are thrown into the ditch.

比犧尊於溝中之斷
則美惡有間矣
Compare the sacrificial bowls with the chips in the ditch,
and you will find them far apart in beauty and ugliness;
其於失性一也
yet they are alike in having lost their inborn nature.

跖與曾史
行義有間矣
然其失性均也
Robber Zhi, Zeng, and Shi
are far apart in deeds and righteousness,
and yet they are the same in having lost their inborn nature.

且夫失性有五
There are five conditions under which the inborn nature is lost.

一曰五色亂目
使目不明
One: when the five colors confuse the eye
and cause the eyesight to be unclear.

二曰五聲亂耳
使耳不聰
Two: when the five notes confuse the ear
and cause the hearing to be unclear.

三曰五臭薰鼻
困惾中顙
Three: when the five odors stimulate the nose
and produce weariness and congestion in the forehead.

四曰五味濁口
使口厲爽
Four: when the five flavors dull the mouth,
causing the sense of taste to be impaired and lifeless.

五曰趣舍滑心
使性飛揚
Five: when likes and dislikes unsettle the mind
and cause the inborn nature to become volatile and flighty.

此五者皆生之害也
These five all are a danger to life.

而楊墨乃始離跂
自以為得
And yet the followers of Yangzi and Mozi go striding around,
thinking they have really gotten hold of something.[^12-21]

[^12-21]: On Yangzi and Mozi, see p. 61, n. 7.
They preached acceptance and rejection, repectively, of sensual pleasure.

非吾所謂得也
This is not what I call getting hold of something.

***

夫得者困
可以為得乎
If what you have gotten has gotten you into trouble,
then can you really be said to have gotten something?

則鳩鴞之在於籠也
亦可以為得矣
If so, then the pigeons and doves in their cage
have also gotten hold of something.

且夫趣舍
聲色
以柴其內
With likes and dislikes,
sounds and colors,
you cripple what is on the inside;
皮弁
鷸冠
搢笏
紳修以約其外
with leather caps and snipe-feathered bonnets,
batons stuck in belts and sashes trailing,
you cramp what is on the outside.

內支盈於柴柵
外重纆繳
睆睆然在纆繳之中
而自以為得
The inside hemmed in by pickets and pegs,
the outside heaped with wraps and swathes,
and still you stand in this tangle of wraps and swathes
and declare that you have gotten hold of something?

則是罪人交臂歷指
而虎豹在於囊檻
亦可以為得矣
If so, then the condemned men with their chained wrists and manacled fingers,
the tiger and the leopard in their pens and prisons,
have also gotten hold of something![^12-22]

[^12-22]: These last two paragraphs, with their mention of Robber Zhi, Zeng, and Shi,
and discussion of the five notes, flavors, etc.,
are close in thought and terminology to the preceding sections.
Speculation is that they originally belonged to either sec. 9 or sec. 11.
