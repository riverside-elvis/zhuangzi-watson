## 22 知北遊 Knowledge Wandered North

### Knowledge asks three questions and the Yellow Emperor answers

知北遊於玄水之上
Knowledge wandered north to the banks of the Black Waters,
登隱弅之丘
climbed the Knoll of Hidden Heights,
而適遭無為謂焉
and there by chance came upon Do-Nothing-Say-Nothing.

知謂無為謂曰
Knowledge said to Do-Nothing-Say-Nothing,
予欲有問乎若
"There are some things I'd like to ask you.

何思何慮則知道
What sort of pondering, what sort of cogitation does it take to know the Way?

何處何服則安道
What sort of surroundings, what sort of practices does it take to find rest in the Way?

何從何道則得道
What sort of path, what sort of procedure will get me to the Way?"

***

三問而無為謂不答也
Three questions he asked, but Do-Nothing-Say-Nothing didn't answer.

非不答
It wasn't that he just didn't answer---
不知答也
he didn't know how to answer!

***

知不得問
Knowledge, failing to get any answer,
反於白水之南
returned to the White Waters of the south,
登狐闋之丘
climbed the summit of Dubiety Dismissed,
而睹狂屈焉
and there caught sight of Wild-and-Witless.

知以之言也問乎狂屈
Knowledge put the same questions to Wild-and-Witless.

狂屈曰唉予知之
"Ah---I know!" said Wild-and-Witless.

將語若
"And I'm going to tell you."

中欲言而忘其所欲言
But just as he was about to say something,
he forgot what it was he was about to say.

***

知不得問
Knowledge, failing to get any answer,
反於帝宮
returned to the imperial palace,
見黃帝而問焉
where he was received in audience by the Yellow Emperor, and posed his questions.

黃帝曰
The Yellow Emperor said,
無思無慮始知道
"Only when there is no pondering and no cogitation will you get to know the Way.

無處無服始安道
Only when you have no surroundings and follow no practices will you find rest in the Way.

無從無道始得道
Only when there is no path and no procedure can you get to the Way."

***

知問黃帝曰
Knowledge said to the Yellow Emperor,
我與若知之
"You and I know,
彼與彼不知也
but those other two that I asked didn't know.

其孰是邪
Which of us is right, I wonder?"

***

黃帝曰
The Yellow Emperor said,
彼無為謂真是也
"Do-Nothing-Say-Nothing---he's the one who is truly right.

狂屈似之
Wild-and-Witless appears to be so.

我與汝終不近也
But you and I in the end are nowhere near it.

夫知者不言
Those who know do not speak;
言者不知
those who speak do not know.

故聖人行不言之教
Therefore the sage practices the teaching that has no words.[^22-1]

[^22-1]: This and the sentence that precedes it appear in _Daodejing_ II and LVI, respectively.

道不可致
The Way cannot be brought to light;
德不可至
its virtue cannot be forced to come.

仁可為也
But benevolence---you can put that into practice;
義可虧也
you can discourse[^22-2] on righteousness,
禮相偽也
you can dupe one another with rites.

[^22-2]: Following the interpretation of Ma Xulun.

故曰
So it is said,
失道而後德
When the Way was lost, then there was virtue;
失德而後仁
when virtue was lost, then there was benevolence;
失仁而後義
when benevolence was lost, then there was righteousness;
失義而後禮
when righteousness was lost, then there were rites.

禮者道之華而亂之首也
Rites are the frills of the Way and the forerunners of disorder.[^22-3]

[^22-3]: The sentence is nearly identical with parts of _Daodejing_ XXXVIII.

故曰
So it is said,
為道者日損
He who practices the Way does less every day,
損之又損之以至於無為
does less and goes on doing less until he reaches the point where he does nothing;
無為而無不為也
does nothing and yet there is nothing that is not done.[^22-4]

[^22-4]: Identical with parts of _Daodejing_ XLVIII.

今已為物也
Now that we've already become 'things,'
欲復歸根
if we want to return again to the Root,
不亦難乎
I'm afraid we'll have a hard time of it!

其易也
The Great Man---
其唯大人乎
he's the only one who might find it easy.

***

生也死之徒
"Life is the companion of death;
死也生之始
death is the beginning of life.

孰知其紀
Who understands their workings?

人之生氣之聚也
Man's life is a coming-together of breath.

聚則為生
If it comes together, there is life;
散則為死
if it scatters, there is death.

若死生為徒
And if life and death are companions to each other,
吾又何患
then what is there for us to be anxious about?

***

故萬物一也
"The ten thousand things are really one.

是其所美者為神奇
We look on some as beautiful because they are rare or unearthly;
其所惡者為臭腐
we look on others as ugly because they are foul and rotten.

臭腐復化為神奇
But the foul and rotten may turn into the rare and un-earthly,
神奇復化為臭腐
and the rare and unearthly may turn into the foul and rotten.

故曰
So it is said,
通天下一氣耳
You have only to comprehend the one breath that is the world.

聖人故貴一
The sage never ceases to value oneness."

***

知謂黃帝曰
Knowledge said to the Yellow Emperor,
吾問無為謂
"I asked Do-Nothing-Say-Nothing,
無為謂不應我
and he didn't reply to me.

非不我應
It wasn't that he merely didn't reply to me---
不知應我也
he didn't know how to reply to me.

吾問狂屈
I asked Wild-and-Witless,
狂屈中欲告我
and he was about to explain to me,
而不我告
though he didn't explain anything.

非不我告
It wasn't that he wouldn't explain to me---
中欲告
而忘之也
but when he was about to explain,
he forgot what it was.

今予問乎若
Now I have asked you,
若知之
and you know the answer.

奚故不近
Why, then, do you say that you are nowhere near being right?"

***

黃帝曰
The Yellow Emperor said,
彼其真是也
"Do-Nothing-Say-Nothing is the one who is truly right---
以其不知也
because he doesn't know.

此其似之也
Wild-and-Witless appears to be so---
以其忘之也
because he forgets.

予與若終不近也
But you and I in the end are nowhere near it---
以其知之也
because we know."

狂屈聞之
Wild-and-Witless heard of the incident
以黃帝為知言
and concluded that the Yellow Emperor knew what he was talking about.

---

### What may be perceived in heaven

天地有大美而不言
Heaven and earth have their great beauties but do not speak of them;
四時有明法而不議
the four seasons have their clear-marked regularity but do not discuss it;
萬物有成理而不說
the ten thousand things have their principles of growth but do not expound them.

聖人者原天地之美
The sage seeks out the beauties of Heaven and earth
而達萬物之理
and masters the principles of the ten thousand things.

是故至人無為
Thus it is that the Perfect Man does not act,
大聖不作
the Great Sage does not move---
觀於天地之謂也
they have perceived [the Way of] Heaven and earth, we may say.

今彼神明至精
This Way, whose spiritual brightness is of the greatest purity,
與彼百化
joins with others in a hundred transformations.

物已死生方圓
Already things are living or dead, round or square;
莫知其根也
no one can comprehend their source,
扁然而萬物
yet here are the ten thousand things in all their stir and bustle,
自古以固存
just as they have been since ancient times.

六合為巨未離其內
Things as vast as the Six Realms have never passed beyond the border [of the Way];
秋豪為小待之成體
things as tiny as an autumn hair must wait for it to achieve bodily form.

天下莫不沈浮終身不故
There is nothing in the world that does not bob and sink to the end of its days, lacking fixity.

陰陽四時運行
The yin and yang, the four seasons follow one another in succession,
各得其序
each keeping to its proper place.

惛然若亡而存
Dark and hidden, [the Way] seems not to exist, and yet it is there;
油然不形而神
lush and unbounded, it possesses no form but only spirit;
萬物畜而不知
the ten thousand things are shepherded by it, though they do not understand it---
此之謂本根
this is what is called the Source, the Root.

可以觀於天矣
This is what may be perceived in Heaven.

### Nie Que asks Piyi about the Way

齧缺問道乎被衣
Nie Que asked Piyi about the Way.

被衣曰
Piyi said,
若正汝形
"Straighten up your body,
一汝視
unify your vision,
天和將至
and the harmony of Heaven will come to you.

攝汝知
Call in your knowledge,
一汝度
unify your bearing,
神將來舍
and the spirits will come to dwell with you.

德將為汝美
Virtue will be your beauty,
道將為汝居
the Way will be your home,
汝瞳焉如新出之犢
and stupid as a newborn calf,
而無求其故
you will not try to find out the reason why."

***

言未卒齧缺睡寐
Before he had finished speaking, however, Nie Que fell sound asleep.

被衣大說
行歌而去之
Piyi, immensely pleased, left and walked away, singing this song:

曰形若槁骸
Body like a withered corpse,
心若死灰
mind like dead ashes,
真其實知
true in the realness of knowledge,
不以故自持
not one to go searching for reasons,
媒媒晦晦
dim dim, dark dark,
無心而不可與謀
mindless, you cannot consult with him,
彼何人哉
what kind of man is this?

---

### Shun asks Cheng about gaining possession of the Way

舜問乎丞曰
Shun asked Cheng,
道可得而有乎
"Is it possible to gain possession of the Way?"

曰汝身非汝有也
"You don't even have possession of your own body---
汝何得有夫道
how could you possibly gain possession of the Way!"

***

舜曰吾身非吾有也孰有之哉
"If I don't have possession of my own body, then who does?" said Shun.

***

曰是天地之委形也
"It is a form lent you by Heaven and earth.

生非汝有
You do not have possession of life---
是天地之委和也
it is a harmony lent by Heaven and earth.

性命非汝有
You do not have possession of your inborn nature and fate---
是天地之委順也
they are contingencies lent by Heaven and earth.

孫子非汝有
You do not have possession of your sons and grandsons---
是天地之委蛻也
they are castoff skins lent by Heaven and earth.

故行不知所往
So it is best to walk without knowing where you are going,
處不知所持
stay home without knowing what you are guarding,
食不知所味
eat without knowing what you are tasting.

天地之強陽氣也
All is the work of the Powerful Yang[^22-5] in the world.

[^22-5]: See sec. 27, p. 237.

又胡可得而有邪
How, then, could it be possible to gain possession of anything?"

---

### Confucius asks Lao Dan about the Perfect Way

孔子問於老聃曰
Confucius said to Lao Dan,
今日晏閒
"Today you seem to have a moment of leisure---
敢問至道
may I venture to ask about the Perfect Way?"

***

老聃曰
Lao Dan said,
汝齊戒
"You must fast and practice austerities,
疏𤅢而心
cleanse and purge your mind,
澡雪而精神
wash and purify your inner spirit,
掊擊而知
destroy and do away with your knowledge.

夫道窅然難言哉
The Way is abstruse and difficult to describe.

將為汝言其崖略
But I will try to give you a rough outline of it.

***

夫昭昭生於冥冥
"The bright and shining is born out of deep darkness;
有倫生於無形
the ordered is born out of formlessness;
精神生於道
pure spirit is born out of the Way.

形本生於精
The body is born originally from this purity,[^22-6]
而萬物以形相生
and the ten thousand things give bodily form to one another through the process of birth.

[^22-6]: Or seminal fluid; see p. 121, n. 2.

故九竅者胎生
Therefore those with nine openings in the body are born from the womb;
八竅者卵生
those with eight openings are born from eggs.

其來無跡
[In the case of the Way,] there is no trace of its coming,
其往無崖
no limit to its going.

無門無房
Gateless, roomless,
四達之皇皇也
it is airy and open as the highways of the four directions.

邀於此者四肢彊
He who follows along with it will be strong in his four limbs,
思慮恂達
keen and penetrating in intellect,
耳目聰明
sharp eared, bright eyed,
其用心不勞
wielding his mind without wearying it,
其應物無方
responding to things without prejudice.

天不得不高
Heaven cannot help but be high;
地不得不廣
earth cannot help but be broad;
日月不得不行
the sun and moon cannot help but revolve;
萬物不得不昌
the ten thousand things cannot help but flourish.

此其道與
Is this not the Way?

***

且夫博之不必知
"Breadth of learning does not necessarily mean knowledge;
辯之不必慧
eloquence does not necessarily mean wisdom---
聖人以斷之矣
therefore the sage rids himself of these things.

若夫益之而不加益
That which can be increased without showing any sign of increase;
損之而不加損者
that which can be diminished without suffering any diminution---
聖人之所保也
that is what the sage holds fast to.

淵淵乎其若海
Deep, unfathomable, it is like the sea;
魏魏乎其終則復始也
tall and craggy,[^22-7] it ends only to begin again,
運量萬物而不匱
transporting and weighing the ten thousand things without ever failing them.

[^22-7]: Probably the words "it is like the mountains,"
which would complete the parallelism, have dropped out at this point.

則君子之道彼其外與
The 'Way of the gentleman,' [which you preach,] is mere superficiality, is it not?

萬物皆往資焉而不匱
But what the ten thousand things all look to for sustenance, what never fails them---
此其道與
is this not the real Way?

***

中國有人焉
"Here is a man of the Middle Kingdom,
非陰非陽
neither yin nor yang,
處於天地之閒
living between heaven and earth.

直且為人
For a brief time only, he will be a man,
將反於宗
and then he will return to the Ancestor.

自本觀之
Look at him from the standpoint of the Source,
生者暗醷物也
and his life is a mere gathering together of breath.

雖有壽夭
And whether he dies young or lives to a great old age,
相去幾何
the two fates will scarcely differ---
須臾之說也
a matter of a few moments, you might say.

奚足以為堯桀之是非
How, then, is it worth deciding that Yao is good and Jie is bad?

***

果蓏有理
"The fruits of trees and vines have their patterns and principles.

人倫雖難所以相齒
Human relationships, too, difficult as they are, have their relative order and precedence.

聖人遭之而不違
The sage, encountering them, does not go against them;
過之而不守
passing beyond, he does not cling to them.

調而應之德也
To respond to them in a spirit of harmony---this is virtue;
偶而應之道也
to respond to them in a spirit of fellowship---this is the Way.

帝之所興王之所起也
Thus it is that emperors have raised themselves up and kings have climbed to power.

***

人生天地之間
若白駒之過郤
"Man's life between heaven and earth
is like the passing of a white colt glimpsed through a crack in the wall---
忽然而已
whoosh!---and that's the end.

注然勃然
Overflowing, starting forth,
莫不出焉
there is nothing that does not come out;
油然漻然
gliding away, slipping into silence,
莫不入焉
there is nothing that does not go back in.

已化而生
Having been transformed, things find themselves alive;
又化而死
another transformation and they are dead.

生物哀之
Living things grieve over it,
人類悲之
mankind mourns.

解其天弢
But it is like the untying of the Heaven-lent bow-bag,
墮其天𧙍
the unloading of the Heavenlent satchel---
紛乎宛乎
a yielding, a mild mutation,
魂魄將往
and the soul and spirit are on their way,
乃身從之
the body following after,
乃大歸乎
on at last to the Great Return.

***

不形之形
"The formless moves to the realm of form;
形之不形
the formed moves back to the realm of formlessness.

是人之所同知也
This all men alike understand.

非將至之所務也
But it is not something to be reached by striving.

此眾人之所同論也
The common run of men all alike debate how to reach it.

彼至則不論
But those who have reached it do not debate,
論則不至
and those who debate have not reached it.

明見無值
Those who peer with bright eyes will never catch sight of it.

辯不若默
Eloquence is not as good as silence.

道不可聞
The Way cannot be heard;
聞不若塞
to listen for it is not as good as plugging up your ears.

此之謂大得
This is called the Great Acquisition."

---

### Master Dongguo asks Zhuangzi if the Way exists

東郭子問於莊子曰
Master Dongguo[^22-8] asked Zhuangzi,
所謂道惡乎在
"This thing called the Way---where does it exist?"

[^22-8]: Literally, "East Wall Master,"
perhaps intended to be the same as Master Shun from east of the Wall in sec. 21.

***

莊子曰
Zhuangzi, said,
無所不在
"There's no place it doesn't exist."

***

東郭子曰
"Come," said Master Dongguo,
期而後可
"you must be more specific!"

莊子曰在螻蟻
"It is in the ant."

曰何其下邪
"As low a thing as that?"

曰在稊稗
"It is in the panic grass."

曰何其愈下邪
"But that's lower still!"

曰在瓦甓
"It is in the tiles and shards."

曰何其愈甚邪
"How can it be so low?"

曰在屎溺
"It is in the piss and shit!"

東郭子不應
Master Dongguo made no reply.

***

莊子曰
Zhuangzi said,
夫子之問也固不足質
"Sir, your questions simply don't get at the substance of the matter.

正獲之問於監市履狶也
When Inspector Huo asked the superintendent of the market how to test the fatness of a pig by pressing it with the foot,
每下愈況
he was told that the lower down on the pig you press, the nearer you come to the truth.

汝唯莫必
But you must not expect to find the Way in any particular place---
無乎逃物
there is no thing that escapes its presence!

至道若是
Such is the Perfect Way,
大言亦然
and so too are the truly great words.

周遍咸
'Complete,' 'universal,' 'all-inclusive'---
三者異名同實
these three are different words with the same meaning.

其指一也
All point to a single reality.

***

嘗相與游乎無何有之宮
"Why don't you try wandering with me to the Palace of Not-Even-Anything---
同合而論
identity and concord will be the basis of our discussions,
無所終窮乎
and they will never come to an end, never reach exhaustion.

嘗相與無為乎
Why not join with me in inaction,
澹而靜乎
in tranquil quietude,
漠而清乎
in hushed purity,
調而閒乎
in harmony and leisure?

寥已吾志
Already my will is vacant and blank.

無往焉
I go nowhere
而不知其所至
and don't know how far I've gotten.

去而來
I go and come
而不知其所止
and don't know where to stop.

吾已往來焉
I've already been there and back,
而不知其所終
and I don't know when the journey is done.

彷徨乎馮閎
I ramble and relax in unbordered vastness;
大知入焉
Great Knowledge enters in,
而不知其所窮
and I don't know where it will ever end.

***

物物者與物無際
"That which treats things as things is not limited by things.

而物有際者
Things have their limits---
所謂物際者也
the so-called limits of things.

不際之際
The unlimited moves to the realm of limits;
際之不際者也
the limited moves to the unlimited realm.

謂盈虛衰殺
We speak of the filling and emptying,
the withering and decay of things.

彼為盈虛非盈虛
[The Way] makes them full and empty without itself filling or emptying;
彼為衰殺非衰殺
it makes them wither and decay without itself withering or decaying.

彼為本末非本末
It establishes root and branch but knows no root and branch itself;
彼為積散非積散也
it determines when to store up or scatter but knows no storing or scattering itself."

### Old Longji dies

婀荷甘與神農同學於老龍吉
A Hegan and Shennong were studying together under Old Longji.[^22-9]

[^22-9]: On Shennong, see p. 142. Old Longji's name means Old Dragon Fortune.

神農隱几闔戶晝瞑
Shennong sat leaning on his armrest, the door shut, taking his daily nap,
婀荷甘日中奓戶而入曰
when at midday A Hegan threw open the door, entered, and announced,
老龍死矣
"Old Long is dead!"

***

神農隱几
Shennong, still leaning on the armrest,
擁杖而起
reached for his staff and jumped to his feet.

嚗然放杖而笑曰
Then he dropped the staff with a clatter and began to laugh, saying,
天知予僻陋慢訑
"My Heavensent Master---he knew how cramped and mean, how arrogant and willful I am,
故棄予而死已矣
and so he abandoned me and died.

夫子無所發予之狂言而死矣夫
My Master went off and died without ever giving me any wild words to open up my mind!"

***

弇堈弔聞之曰
Yan Gangdiao, hearing of the incident, said,
夫體道者天下之君子所繫焉
"He who embodies the Way has all the gentlemen of the world flocking to him.

今於道
As far as the Way goes,
秋豪之端
Old Long hadn't gotten hold of a piece as big as the tip of an autumn hair,
萬分未得處一焉
hadn't found his way into one ten-thousandth of it---
而猶知藏其狂言而死
but even he knew enough to keep his wild words stored away and to die with them unspoken.

又況夫體道者乎
How much more so, then, in the case of a man who embodies the Way!

視之無形
Look for it, but it has no form;
聽之無聲
listen, but it has no voice.

於人之論者謂之冥冥
Those who discourse on it with other men speak of it as dark and mysterious.

所以論道而非道也
The Way that is discoursed on is not the Way at all!"

***

### Grand Purity tries to understand the Way

於是泰清問乎無窮曰
At this point, Grand Purity asked No-End,
子知道乎
"Do *you* understand the Way?"

無窮曰吾不知
"I don't understand it," said No-End.

***

又問乎無為無為曰
Then he asked No-Action, and No-Action said,
吾知道
"I understand the Way."

曰子之知道
"You say you understand the Way---
亦有數乎
is there some trick to it?

曰有
"There is."

曰其數若何
"What's the trick?"

***

無為曰
No-Action said,
吾知道之可以貴可以賤
"I understand that the Way can exalt things and can humble them,
可以約可以散
that it can bind them together and can cause them to disperse.[^22-10]

[^22-10]: That is, cause them to be born and to die.

此吾所以知道之數也
This is the trick by which I understand the Way."

***

泰清以之言也
Grand Purity, having received these various answers,
問乎無始曰
went and questioned No-Beginning, saying,
若是則無窮之弗知
"If this is how it is, then between No-End's declaration that he doesn't understand
與無為之知
and No-Action's declaration that he does,
孰是而孰非乎
which is right and which is wrong?"

***

無始曰
No-Beginning said,
不知深矣
"Not to understand is profound;
知之淺矣
to understand is shallow.

弗知內矣
Not to understand is to be on the inside;
知之外矣
to understand is to be on the outside."

***

於是泰清中而歎曰
Thereupon Grand Purity gazed up[^22-11] and sighed, saying,
弗知乃知乎
"Not to understand is to understand?

[^22-11]: Following Xi Tong, I read *yang* in place of *zhong*.

知乃不知乎
To understand is not to understand?

孰知不知之知
Who understands the understanding that does not understand?"

***

無始曰
No-Beginning said,
道不可聞
"The Way cannot be heard;
聞而非也
heard, it is not the Way.

道不可見
The Way cannot be seen;
見而非也
seen, it is not the Way.

道不可言
The Way cannot be described;
言而非也
described, it is not the Way.

知形形之不形乎
That which gives form to the formed is itself formless---can you understand that?

道不當名
There is no name that fits the Way."

***

無始曰
No-Beginning continued,
有問道而應之者不知道也
"He who, when asked about the Way, gives an answer does not understand the Way;
雖問道者亦未聞道
and he who asked about the Way has not really heard the Way explained.

道無問
The Way is not to be asked about,
問無應
and even if it is asked about, there can be no answer.

無問問之是問窮也
To ask about what cannot be asked about is to ask for the sky.

無應應之是無內也
To answer what cannot be answered is to try to split hairs.

以無內待問窮若是者
If the hair splitter waits for the sky asker,[^22-12]
外不觀乎宇宙
then neither will ever perceive the time and space that surround them on the outside
內不知乎太初
or understand the Great Beginning that is within.

[^22-12]: I follow Guo Xiang in the interpretation of the phrase "to ask for the sky,"
that is, to try to measure the immeasurable.
*Neiwu*, that which is so minute there is nothing inside it---
translated here as "to split hairs"---appears in sec. 33, p. 297.

是以不過乎崑崙
Such men can never trek across the Kunlun,
不遊乎太虛
can never wander in the Great Void!"[^22-13]

[^22-13]: The Kunlun, a fabulous range of mountains to the far west where the immortal spirits dwell,
was mentioned on p. 141.

### Bright Dazzlement asks Nonexistence if he exists

光曜問乎無有曰
Bright Dazzlement asked Nonexistence,
夫子有乎
"Sir, do you exist,
其無有乎
or do you not exist?"

光曜不得問
Unable to obtain any answer,
而孰視其狀貌
Bright Dazzlement stared intently at the other's face and form---
窅然空然
all was vacuity and blankness.

終日視之而不見
He stared all day but could see nothing,
聽之而不聞
listened but could hear no sound,
搏之而不得也
stretched out his hand but grasped nothing.

光曜曰至矣
"Perfect!" exclaimed Bright Dazzlement.

其孰能至此乎
"Who can reach such perfection?

予能有無矣
I can conceive of the existence of nonexistence
而未能無無也
but not of the nonexistence of nonexistence.

及為無有矣
Yet this man has reached the stage of the nonexistence of nonexistence.[^22-14]

[^22-14]: I read *wuwu*, following the parallel passage in *Huainanzi*, sec. 2.

何從至此哉
How could I ever reach such perfection!"

### The old buckle maker's way

大馬之捶鉤者年八十矣
The grand marshal's buckle maker was eighty years old,
而不失豪芒
yet he had not lost the tiniest part of his old dexterity.

大馬曰子巧與
The grand marshal said, "What skill you have!

有道與
Is there a special way to this?"

***

曰臣有守也
"I have a way.[^22-15]

[^22-15]: Following Wang Niansun, I read *dao* in place of *shou*; compare the similar passage, p. 147.

臣之年二十
From the time I was twenty,
而好捶鉤
I have loved to forge buckles.

於物無視也
I never look at other things---
非鉤無察也
if it's not a buckle, I don't bother to examine it."

***

是用之者假不用者也
Using this method of deliberately *not* using other things,
以長得其用
he was able, over the years, to get some use out of it.

而況乎
And how much greater would a man be if, by the same method,
無不用者乎
he reached the point where there was nothing that he did not use!

物孰不資焉
All things would come to depend on him.

### Ran Qiu asks Confucius about the time before Heaven and earth

冉求問於仲尼曰
Ran Qiu asked Confucius,
未有天地可知邪
"Is it possible to know anything about the time before Heaven and earth existed?"

***

仲尼曰
Confucius said,
可古猶今也
"It is---the past is the present."

***

冉求失問而退
Ran Qiu, failing to receive any further answer, retired.

明日復見曰
The following day he went to see Confucius again and said,
昔者吾問未有天地可知乎
"Yesterday I asked if it were possible to know anything about the time before Heaven and earth existed,
夫子曰
and you, Master, replied,
可古猶今也
'It is---the past is the present.'

昔者吾昭然
Yesterday that seemed quite clear to me,
今日吾昧然
but today it seems very obscure.

敢問何謂也
May I venture to ask what this means?"

***

仲尼曰
Confucius said,
昔之昭然也神者先受之
"Yesterday it was clear because your spirit took the lead in receiving my words.

今之昧然也
Today, if it seems obscure,
且又為不神者求邪
it is because you are searching for it with something other than spirit, are you not?

無古無今
There is no past and no present,
無始無終
no beginning and no end.

未有子孫而有子孫可乎
Sons and grandsons existed before sons and grandsons existed---may we make such a statement?"

***

冉求未對仲尼曰
Ran Qiu had not replied when Confucius said,
已矣末應矣
"Stop!---don't answer!

不以生生死
Do not use life to give life to death.

不以死死生
Do not use death to bring death to life.[^22-16]

[^22-16]: Compare sec. 6, p. 46:
"That which kills life does not die;
that which gives life to life does not live."

死生有待邪
Do life and death depend on each other?

皆有所一體
Both have in them that which makes them a single body.

有先天地生者物邪
There is that which was born before Heaven and earth, but is it a thing?

物物者非物
That which treats things as things is not a thing.

物出不得先物也
Things that come forth can never precede all other things,
猶其有物也
because there already were things existing then;
猶其有物也無已
and before that, too, there already were things existing---so on without end.

聖人之愛人也
The sage's love of mankind,
終無已者
which never comes to an end,
亦乃取於是者也
is modeled on this principle."

### Yan Yuan asks Confucius how to wander

顏淵問乎仲尼曰
Yan Yuan said to Confucius,
回嘗聞諸夫子曰
"Master, I have heard you say
無有所將
that there should be no going after anything,
無有所迎
no welcoming anything.[^22-17]

[^22-17]: Compare sec. 7, p. 59: "The Perfect Man uses his mind like a mirror---
going after nothing, welcoming nothing, responding but not storing."

回敢問其遊
May I venture to ask how one may wander in such realms?"

***

仲尼曰
Confucius said,
古之人外化而內不化
"The men of old changed on the outside but not on the inside.

今之人內化而外不化
The men of today change on the inside but not on the outside.

與物化者一不化者也
He who changes along with things is identical with him who does not change.

安化
Where is there change?

安不化
Where is there no change?

安與之相靡
Where is there any friction with others?

必與之莫多
Never will he treat others with arrogance.

狶韋氏之囿
But Xiwei had his park,
黃帝之圃
the Yellow Emperor his garden,
有虞氏之宮
Shun his palace,
湯武之室
Tang and Wu their halls.[^22-18]

[^22-18]: The mythical figure Xiwei appeared on p. 45.
The series "park," "garden," "palace," "hall" probably represents a devolution from naturalness
to increasing artificiality and extravagance, though the older interpretation
is that these were the "groves of Academe" of high antiquity.

君子之人
And among gentlemen,
若儒墨者師
there were those like the Confucians and Mohists who became 'teachers.'

故以是非相𩐋也
As a result, people began using their 'rights' and 'wrongs' to push one another around.

而況今之人乎
And how much worse are the men of today!

***

聖人處物不傷物
"The sage lives with things but does no harm to them,
不傷物者物亦不能傷也
and he who does no harm to things cannot in turn be harmed by them.

唯無所傷者為能與人相將迎
Only he who does no harm is qualified to join with other men in 'going after' or 'welcoming.'

***

山林與
"The mountains and forests,
皋壤與
the hills and fields,
使我欣欣然而樂與
fill us with overflowing delight, and we are joyful.

樂未畢也哀又繼之
Our joy has not ended when grief comes trailing it.

哀樂之來吾不能禦
We have no way to bar the arrival of grief and joy,
其去弗能止
no way to prevent them from departing.

悲夫世人
Alas, the men of this world are no more than travelers,
直為物逆旅耳
stopping now at this inn, now at that, all of them run by 'things.'

夫知遇
They know the things they happen to encounter
而不知所不遇
but not those that they have never encountered.

知能能
They know how to do the things they can do,
而不能所不能
but they can't do the things they don't know how to do.

無知無能者
Not to know, not to be able to do---
固人之所不免也
from these, mankind can never escape.

夫務免乎人之所不免者
And yet there are those who struggle to escape from the inescapable---
豈不亦悲哉
can you help but pity them?

至言去言
Perfect speech is the abandonment of speech;
至為去為
perfect action is the abandonment of action.

齊知之所知
To be limited to understanding only what is understood---
則淺矣
this is shallow indeed!"
