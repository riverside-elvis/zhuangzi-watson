## 30 說劍 Discoursing on Swords[^30-1]

[^30-1]: The title may also be interpreted to mean "Delighting in Swords."
Why both meanings are appropriate will become apparent.

昔趙文王喜劍
In ancient times, King Wen of Zhao was fond of swords.

劍士夾門
而客三千餘人
日夜相擊於前
死傷者歲百餘人
Expert swordsmen flocked to his gate,
and more than three thousand of them were supported as guests in his household,
day and night, engaging in bouts in his presence
till the dead and wounded numbered more than a hundred men a year.

好之不厭
如是三年
國衰
諸侯謀之
Yet the king's delight never seemed to wane,
and things went on in this way for three years
while the state sank into decline
and the other feudal lords conspired against it.

***

太子悝患之
募左右曰孰能說王之意止劍士者
賜之千金
The crown prince Kui, distressed at this,
summoned his retainers around him and said, "I will bestow a thousand pieces of gold on any man who can reason with the king
and make him give up these sword fights!"

左右曰莊子當能
"Zhuangzi is the one who can do it," said his retainers.

***

太子乃使人以千金奉莊子
莊子弗受
The crown prince thereupon sent an envoy with a thousand pieces of gold to present to Zhuangzi,
but Zhuangzi refused to accept the gift.

與使者俱往見太子
Instead he accompanied the envoy on his return and went to call on the crown prince.

曰太子何以教周
賜周千金
"What instructions do you have for me,
that you present me with a thousand pieces of gold?" he asked.

***

太子曰聞夫子明聖
謹奉千金以幣從者
"I had heard, sir," said the crown prince, "that you are an enlightened sage,
and I wished in all due respect to offer this thousand in gold as a gift to your attendants.

夫子弗受
悝尚何敢言
But if you refuse to accept it,
then I dare say no more about the matter."

***

莊子曰聞太子所欲用周者
欲絕王之喜好也
Zhuangzi said, "I have heard that the crown prince wishes to employ me
because he hopes I can rid the king of this passion of his.

使臣上說大王
而逆王意
下不當太子
則身刑而死
Now if, in attempting to persuade His Majesty,
I should arouse his anger
and fail to satisfy your hopes,
then I would be sentenced to execution.

周尚安所事金乎
In that case, what use could I make of the gold?

使臣上說大王
下當太子
趙國何求而不得也
And if I should be able to persuade His Majesty
and satisfy your hopes,
then what could I ask for in the whole kingdom of Zhao that would not be granted me?"

***

太子曰然吾王所見唯劍士也
"The trouble is," said the crown prince, "that my father, the king, refuses to see anyone but swordsmen."

***

莊子曰諾
"Fine!" said Zhuangzi.

周善為劍
"I am quite able to handle a sword."

***

太子曰然吾王所見劍士
皆蓬頭
突鬢
垂冠
曼胡之纓
短後之衣
"But the kind of swordsmen my father receives," said the crown prince,
"all have tousled heads
and bristling beards,
wear slouching caps
tied with plain, coarse tassels,
and robes that are cut short behind;
嗔目而語難
they glare fiercely and have difficulty getting out their words.

王乃說之
Men like that he is delighted with!

今夫子必儒服而見王
事必大逆
Now, sir, if you should insist on going to see him in scholarly garb,
the whole affair would go completely wrong from the start."

***

莊子曰請治劍服
"Then allow me to get together the garb of a swords-man," said Zhuangzi.

治劍服三日
乃見太子
After three days, he had his swordsman's costume ready
and went to call on the crown prince.

太子乃與見王
The crown prince and he then went to see the king.

王脫白刃待之
The king, drawing his sword, waited with bare blade in hand.

莊子入殿門不趨
見王不拜
Zhuangzi entered the door of the hall with unhurried steps,
looked at the king but made no bow.

***

王曰子欲何以教寡人
使太子先
The king said, "Now that you have gotten the crown prince to prepare the way for you,
what kind of instruction is it you intend to give me?"

***

曰臣聞大王喜劍
故以劍見王
"I have heard that Your Majesty is fond of swords,
and so I have come with my sword to present myself before you."

王曰子之劍何能禁制
"And what sort of authority does your sword command?" asked the king.

***

曰臣之劍十步一人
千里不留行
"My sword cuts down one man every ten paces,
and for a thousand *li* it never ceases its flailing!"

***

王大悅之曰天下無敵矣
The king, greatly pleased, exclaimed, "You must have no rival in the whole world!"

***

莊子曰夫為劍者示之以虛
開之以利
後之以發
先之以至
Zhuangzi said, "The wielder of the sword makes a display of emptiness,
draws one out with hopes of advantage,
is behind time in setting out,
but beforehand in arriving.[^30-2]

[^30-2]: The sentence is deliberately cryptic and capable of interpretation on a variety of levels.

願得試之
May I be allowed to try what I can do?"

***

王曰夫子休就舍
待命
The king said, "You may leave now, sir, and go to your quarters
to await my command.

令設戲
請夫子
When I am ready to hold the bout,
I will request your presence again."

***

王乃校劍士七日
The king then spent seven days testing the skill of his swordsmen.

死傷者六十餘人
得五六人
使奉劍於殿下
More than sixty were wounded or died in the process,
leaving five or six survivors
who were ordered to present themselves with their swords outside the king's hall.

乃召莊子
王曰今日試使士敦劍
Then the king sent for Zhuangzi,
saying, "Today let us see what happens when you cross swords with these gentlemen."

***

莊子曰望之久矣
Zhuangzi said, "It is what I have long wished for."

***

王曰夫子所御杖
長短何如
"What weapon will you use, sir," asked the king,
"a long sword or a short one?"

***

曰臣之所奉皆可
"I am prepared to use any type at all.

然臣有三劍
It happens that I have three swords---
唯王所用
Your Majesty has only to indicate which you wish me to use.

請先言
而後試
If I may, I will first explain them,
and then put them to the test."

王曰願聞三劍
"Let me hear about your three swords," said the king.

***

曰有天子劍
有諸侯劍
有庶人劍
"There is the sword of the Son of Heaven,
the sword of the feudal lord,
and the sword of the commoner."

王曰天子之劍何如
"What is the sword of the Son of Heaven like?" asked the king.

***

曰天子之劍
"The sword of the Son of Heaven?

以燕谿石城為鋒
The Valley of Yan and the Stone Wall are its point;
齊岱為鍔
Qi and Dai its blade;
晉魏為脊
Jin and Wey its spine;
周宋為鐔
Zhou and Song its sword guard;
韓魏為夾
Han and Wei its hilt.[^30-3]

[^30-3]: These all are feudal states or strategic places of northern China surrounding the state of Zhao.

包以四夷
The four barbarian tribes enwrap it;
裹以四時
the four seasons enfold it;
繞以渤海
the seas of Bo surround it;
帶以常山
the mountains of Chang girdle it.

制以五行
The five elements govern it;
論以刑德
the demands of punishment and favor direct it.

開以陰陽
持以春夏
行以秋冬
It is brought forth in accordance with the yin and yang,
held in readiness in spring and summer,
wielded in autumn and winter.

此劍直之無前
Thrust it forward, and there is nothing that will stand before it;
舉之無上
raise it on high, and there is nothing above it;
案之無下
press it down, and there is nothing beneath it;
運之無旁
whirl it about, and there is nothing surrounding it.
上決浮雲
Above, it cleaves the drifting clouds;
下絕地紀
below, it severs the sinews of the earth.

此劍一用
匡諸侯
天下服矣
When this sword is once put to use,
the feudal lords return to their former obedience,
and the whole world submits.

此天子之劍也
This is the sword of the Son of Heaven."

***

文王芒然自失
King Wen, dumbfounded, appeared to be at an utter loss.

曰諸侯之劍何如
Then he said, "What is the sword of the feudal lord like?"

***

曰諸侯之劍
"The sword of the feudal lord?

以知勇士為鋒
以清廉士為鍔
以賢良士為脊
以忠聖士為鐔
以豪桀士為夾
It has wise and brave men for its point,
men of purity and integrity for its blade,
men of worth and goodness for its spine,
men of loyalty and sageliness for its sword guard,
heroes and prodigies for its hilt.

此劍值之亦無前
This sword too, thrust forward, meets nothing before it;
舉之亦無上
raised, it encounters nothing above;
案之亦無下
pressed down, it encounters nothing beneath it;
運之亦無旁
whirled about, it meets nothing surrounding it.

上法圓天
以順三光
Above, it takes its model from the roundness of heaven,
following along with the three luminous bodies of the sky.[^30-4]

[^30-4]: The stars collectively make up the third luminous body.

下法方地
以順四時
Below, it takes its model from the squareness of earth,
following along with the four seasons.

中和民意
以安四鄉
In the middle realm, it brings harmony to the wills of the people
and peace to the four directions.

此劍一用
如雷霆之震也
This sword, once put into use,
is like the crash of a thunderbolt:
四封之內
無不賓服
none within the four borders of the state
will fail to bow down in submission;
而聽從君命者矣
none will fail to heed and obey the commands of the ruler.

此諸侯之劍也
This is the sword of the feudal lord."

***

王曰庶人之劍何如
The king said, "What is the sword of the commoner like?"

***

曰庶人之劍
"The sword of the commoner?

蓬頭
突鬢
垂冠
曼胡之纓
短後之衣
瞋目而語難
相擊於前
It is used by men with tousled heads
and bristling beards,
with slouching caps
tied with plain, coarse tassels
and robes cut short behind,
who glare fiercely and speak with great difficulty,
who slash at one another in Your Majesty's presence.

上斬頸領
Above, it lops off heads and necks;
下決肝肺
below, it splits open livers and lungs.

此庶人之劍
無異於鬥雞
一旦命已絕矣
Those who wield this sword of the commoner
are no different from fighting cocks---
any morning their lives may be cut off.

無所用於國事
They are of no use in the administration of the state.

***

今大王有天子之位
而好庶人之劍
"Now Your Majesty occupies the position of a Son of Heaven,
and yet you show this fondness for the sword of the commoner.[^30-5]

[^30-5]: The state of Zhao, situated in north central China,
was never very powerful, and its king, only one among many feudal rulers of the time,
in no sense occupied anything that could be called "the position of a Son of Heaven."
If the writer has not abandoned all pretense at historicity,
he must mean that if the king of Zhao were to rule wisely,
he might in time gain sufficient power and prestige to become a contender for the position of Son of Heaven.

臣竊為大王薄之
If I may be so bold, I think it rather unworthy of you!

***

王乃牽而上殿
宰人上食
王三環之
The king thereupon led Zhuangzi up into his hall,
where the royal butler came forward with trays of food,
but the king merely paced round and round the room.

***

莊子曰大王安坐定氣
"Your Majesty should seat yourself at ease and calm your spirits," said Zhuangzi.

劍事已畢奏矣
"The affair of the sword is all over and finished!"

***

於是文王不出宮三月
劍士皆服斃其處也
After this, King Wen did not emerge from his palace for three months,
and his swordsmen all committed suicide in their quarters.
