A mashup of Burton Watson's _The Complete works of Zhuangzi_ (2013)
and [ctext](https://ctext.org/zhuangzi) _Xu Guyi congshu 續古逸叢書_.

Watson based his translation on Liu Wendian's _Zhuangzi bu zheng 莊子補正_,
but except for the difference between simplified (_Zhuangzi bu zheng_) and traditional (_Xu Guyi congshu_),
their base texts seem practically identical. I've added notes where I think they differ.
